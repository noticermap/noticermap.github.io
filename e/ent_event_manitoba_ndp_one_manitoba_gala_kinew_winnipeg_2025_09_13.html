Manitoba NDP One Manitoba Gala with Premier Wab Kinew, Winnipeg (set for May 31, 2025; postponed; rescheduled to Sept 13, 2025)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Manitoba NDP's own event page (Wayback, Apr 28, 2025) advertised its flagship One Manitoba Gala Dinner with Wab Kinew for Saturday May 31 [2025] at the Victoria Inn, Winnipeg, with a VIP reception with Kinew at 5 p.m. A June 2025 capture of the page gives Saturday Sept 13 and says the event 'had been postponed due to the provincial state of emergency. Any tickets purchased before June will be honoured for the rescheduled date.' Ticket tiers on the page, $150 to $3,000, are listed in money. Organizer's own claim. I found no news coverage. A Nisichawayasihk Cree Nation schedule page lists 'Saturday September 13, 2025: One Manitoba Gala'.
