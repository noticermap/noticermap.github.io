Ontario Ombudsman twice declines to review the OPP commissioner hiring (Taverner); the deputy commissioner who sought the review, Brad Blair, is fired (Dec 2018-Mar 2019)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In December 2018 OPP deputy commissioner Brad Blair asked Ombudsman Paul Dubé to review the hiring process that chose Toronto police Supt. Ron Taverner, a longtime friend of Premier Doug Ford, as OPP commissioner. Dubé twice declined, on the basis that the appointment was ultimately a cabinet decision outside his jurisdiction. Blair applied to the Divisional Court (Blair v. The Ombudsman of Ontario, 2019 ONSC 369; an expedited hearing was denied). On March 4, 2019 Blair was fired. Minister Sylvia Jones said he had released confidential OPP information; the termination was recommended by Deputy Minister Mario Di Tommaso, who sat on the hiring panel, and approved by the Public Service Commission (CBC). Taverner later withdrew. Stopped (as reported): Ombudsman declined jurisdiction (twice); court application not expedited; requester dismissed by revocation of appointment (by: Ontario Ombudsman (declined); Deputy Minister / Public Service Commission and cabinet (dismissal)).
