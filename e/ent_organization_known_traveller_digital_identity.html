Known Traveller Digital Identity (KTDI) pilot

Public records ✓Verified in public records

WEF-convened Canada–Netherlands digital travel-identity pilot. Announced at Davos on 2018-01-25 by Navdeep Bains, then Minister of Innovation, Science and Economic Development, on behalf of Transport Minister Marc Garneau. Launched 2019-06-26 at Montreal airport with Air Canada, KLM, YUL Montreal-Trudeau, Toronto Pearson, Schiphol, Accenture, Vision-Box and Idemia. The Dutch government told its parliament on 2022-02-24 that implementation was very uncertain; Transport Canada later told the Financial Post that pilot activities had not restarted. A $105 million figure reported by the Financial Post was not checked against a federal primary record in this pass.
