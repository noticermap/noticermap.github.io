Q-850 (Scott Duvall, June 18, 2021) asked for details of all reports, studies, evaluations and papers produced for the government by McKinsey and Company since Jan 1, 2016 (date, title, summary of recommendations, contract value and more). It remained 'Awaiting response' when the 43-2 session ended Aug 15, 2021; the page states: 'The government is no longer required to respond to this question.' What stopped it: The session ended (Aug 15, 2021) before an answer was tabled.
