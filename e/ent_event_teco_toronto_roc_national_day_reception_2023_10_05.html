Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Toronto reception for the ROC (Taiwan) 112th National Day (Oct 5, 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Epoch Times reports that the Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Toronto hosted an Oct 5, 2023 event for the ROC's 112th National Day. Liberal MP Judy Sgro (chair of the parliamentary Canada-Taiwan Friendship Group) and Independent MP Kevin Vuong spoke; Ontario Education Minister Stephen Lecce and PC MPPs Anthony Leardi, Brian Saunderson, Ernie Hardeman, Nolan Quinn and Robin Martin attended.
