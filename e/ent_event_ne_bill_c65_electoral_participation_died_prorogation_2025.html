Bill C-65 (Electoral Participation Act) died at prorogation (2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Government Bill C-65 (Electoral Participation Act), sponsored by the Minister of Public Safety, Democratic Institutions and Intergovernmental Affairs, last status 'At consideration in committee in the House of Commons' (Second reading and referral to committee June 19, 2024). The session ended with prorogation on January 6, 2025, so the bill did not proceed further in the 44-1 session. What stopped it: Prorogation of Parliament on January 6, 2025 ended the session with the bill unfinished.
