No independent advisory body on police surveillance technology after ETHI Report 7 (2023)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

ETHI Report 7 Rec 8 called for an independent advisory body to set national standards on new technology used by police. The government response pointed instead to the RCMP's internal National Technology Onboarding Program (NTOP). What stopped it: The response substituted the RCMP-internal NTOP for an independent body.
