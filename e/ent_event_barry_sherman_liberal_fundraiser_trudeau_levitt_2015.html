Liberal fundraiser hosted by Apotex chairman Barry Sherman featuring Justin Trudeau and candidate Michael Levitt, Toronto (Aug 26, 2015)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

A $1,500-a-ticket Liberal fundraiser hosted by Apotex chairman Barry Sherman at his home featured Justin Trudeau (then Liberal leader) and candidate Michael Levitt. Democracy Watch complained; the federal lobbying commissioner opened an investigation on Jan 18, 2017 and ceased it after Sherman's death (letter from Commissioner Nancy Bélanger).
