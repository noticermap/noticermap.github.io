Release of the CRCC's Colten Boushie report was held up nearly 11 months awaiting the RCMP Commissioner's response

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The CRCC sent its interim report on the RCMP's investigation of Colten Boushie's 2016 death to Commissioner Brenda Lucki on Jan. 21, 2020. The final report cannot be released until the Commissioner responds, and that response did not come until Dec. 4, 2020, beyond the six-month target reported by CBC and the Globe. The final report was released Jan. 7, 2021. Stopped (as reported): Commissioner's response arrived Dec. 4, 2020; final report Jan. 7, 2021. The Globe reported RCMP backlogs prevented release (by: RCMP Commissioner's delayed response (Brenda Lucki)).
