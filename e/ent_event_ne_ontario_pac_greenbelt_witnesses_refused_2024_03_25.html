Ontario PAC greenbelt witnesses refused (2024-03-25)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

France Gélinas moved that the Committee authorize inviting additional witnesses for its review of the Auditor General's Special Report on Changes to the Greenbelt, including a Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry representative involved with repeal of the Duffins Rouge Agricultural Preserve Act, three named individuals, and ministry witnesses for the 2023 government advertising review. The motion was lost (Ayes: Gélinas; Nays: Bouma, Crawford, Sandhu, Skelly, Laura Smith). At the May 6, 2024 hearing MPP Jeff Burch called it unfortunate that government members did not extend an invitation to MNRF officials. What stopped it: Recorded vote 1-5; Chair declared the motion lost. Later hearing records that MNRF officials were not invited.
