PESRA Part II never brought into force

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Part II of the Parliamentary Employment and Staff Relations Act (R.S.C. 1985, c. 33 (2nd Supp.)), 'Standard Hours, Wages, Leave, Etc.', is shown as not in force on the Justice Laws site and is listed as not in force on Dec 31, 2024 and Dec 31, 2025 in the Statutes Repeal Act annual reports (tabled May 28, 2025 and Feb 2-3, 2026). What stopped it: Never brought into force; still listed under the Statutes Repeal Act as not in force.
