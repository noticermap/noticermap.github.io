Ontario's first Chief Scientist, Molly Shoichet, dismissed and the position's orders in council revoked; no successor named (June-July 2018)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The position of Special Advisor, Chief Scientist was created by O.C. 2242/2017, and Molly Shoichet was appointed by O.C. 2243/2017 (Nov 17, 2017). Her remuneration O.C. 2461/2017 shows it was 'Revoked by: Order in Council 988/2018', a Premier's order of June 29, 2018 revoking a list of orders. On July 3-5, 2018 Shoichet was told she was let go. The Premier's spokesperson said the government would find a 'suitable and qualified replacement' (CBC), but no successor appointment was found. Stopped (as reported): O.C. 988/2018 revoked the orders; Chief Scientist dismissed (by: Premier and President of the Council (O.C. 988/2018)).
