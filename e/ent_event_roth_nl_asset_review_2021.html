Newfoundland and Labrador provincial asset review by Rothschild & Co (2021-2022; Phase II 2023)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Province engaged Rothschild & Co for an independent review of all provincial assets (focus: oil and gas holdings, NL Liquor Corporation, registries, Marble Mountain). Cost about $5 million (Finance Minister Siobhan Coady, per CBC). Report delivered early April 2022 and withheld in full as a cabinet record under ATIPPA s.27. On 2023-03-17 the province announced Phase II on its offshore oil and gas interests, 'being led by Rothschild & Co.' (NL Hydro).
