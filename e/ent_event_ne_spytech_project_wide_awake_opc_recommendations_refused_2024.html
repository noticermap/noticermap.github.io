RCMP did not agree to OPC recommendations on Project Wide Awake (2024)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

After MP Charlie Angus's 2020 complaint, the OPC recommended the RCMP stop collecting personal information from login-protected sources via Babel X and fix its public transparency (s.11 personal information bank descriptions). 'The RCMP did not agree to implement the recommendation, and therefore, we consider this matter to not be resolved.' The transparency issue was found 'well-founded and not resolved', and the RCMP did not commit to implementing its own PIA's recommendations within 12 months. What stopped it: The RCMP refused the OPC recommendation on Babel X login-protected sources and did not resolve the transparency finding.
