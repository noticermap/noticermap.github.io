Alberta Investment Management Corporation

Breach Public records

Crown corporation of the Province of Alberta that manages investments for Alberta public-sector pension plans, endowments and government funds, including the Heritage Savings Trust Fund. Its directors are appointed by Order in Council.

Also known as: AIMCo, Alberta Investment Management Corp, Alberta Investment Management Corp.
