Buckingham Palace financial-sector dinner hosted by Prince Andrew (March 2014)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

A private dinner at Buckingham Palace in mid-March 2014 for about 20 financial-sector figures, paid for by Prince Andrew, as confirmed to the Daily Mail by a Palace spokesman (2014-03-18/19). A Bank of England spokesman said Mark Carney, then Governor, was invited by the Duke of York and that it was Carney’s first such event. Other attendees were reported to include figures from J.P. Morgan, Barclays, HSBC and ICAP. Andrew had resigned as UK trade envoy in 2011 over his ties to Jeffrey Epstein. Attendance is not a finding of wrongdoing.
