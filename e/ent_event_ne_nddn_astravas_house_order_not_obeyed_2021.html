Zita Astravas did not appear before NDDN as ordered by the House (2021)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The House order of March 25, 2021 required Zita Astravas to appear before the National Defence committee on April 6, 2021. At NDDN meeting 23 that day, Minister of National Defence Harjit Sajjan appeared instead; James Bezan said this 'doesn't absolve Zita Astravas from appearing at committee'. NDDN meeting 31 minutes record a Bezan motion reciting that she was invited (Mar 8) and ordered by the House (Apr 6) and 'did not appear on either occasion'; an amendment deleting paragraph (b) carried 7-4 and the minutes show no final vote before adjournment. What stopped it: She did not appear; the Minister appeared instead. A later motion to pursue her appearance was amended and not brought to a final vote in the recorded meeting.
