Quebec's Bill 39 (mixed-member proportional voting system with a 2022 referendum) passes second reading 93-28, then is dropped; no referendum held (2019-2021)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The CAQ had committed in 2018 to reform the voting system (Radio-Canada). Minister Sonia LeBel tabled Bill 39, Loi établissant un nouveau mode de scrutin, on September 25, 2019. It proposed a mixed system with a regional proportional component and a referendum at the 2022 election. The Commission des institutions held consultations in January and February 2020, and the principle was adopted on October 8, 2020 by 93 to 28 (Assemblée nationale). On April 28, 2021 LeBel said there was not enough time to pass the 290-clause bill before a 2022 referendum. In December 2021 Premier François Legault said the government would not call the bill again (Journal de Québec). Detailed study never concluded. Stopped (as reported): Government declared no referendum in 2022 and abandoned the bill; it was never passed (by: Government of Quebec (CAQ): Premier François Legault, Minister Sonia LeBel).
