Ports pacp random exam fee recommendation rejected (2009)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

After the Auditor General's 2007 CBSA audit, PACP recommended that carriers chosen for random inspections not pay examination fees. The government accepted this 'only in part' and kept the fee process. In 2022 CBSA's own audit found 'the prohibitive cost of examinations' still shaped marine targeting. What stopped it: 'The Government accepts the fourth Committee recommendation only in part. ... it has concluded that the process of levying examination fees will not be changed.'
