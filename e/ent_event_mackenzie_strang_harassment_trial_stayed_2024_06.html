N.S. harassment trial of Jeremy MacKenzie and Morgan Guptill stayed for delay (Jun 2024)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Charged in 2022 over a March 2022 protest outside the home of N.S. chief medical officer of health Dr. Robert Strang, the pair were due for trial the following week; provincial court Judge Jill Hartlen stayed proceedings for exceeding the 18-month ceiling. She had earlier found no evidence police acted to "improperly stifle political activity" (CBC). Separate Saskatchewan gun charges were stayed in 2023 (CBC).
