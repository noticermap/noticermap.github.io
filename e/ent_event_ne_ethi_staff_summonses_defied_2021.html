ETHI-summoned ministerial staff did not appear (2021)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On March 25, 2021 the House adopted (Division No. 80, 181-153) Michael Barrett's motion, as amended, ordering Rick Theis, Amitpal Singh and Ben Chin to appear before ETHI (and Zita Astravas before NDDN) on WE Charity / Canada Student Service Grant matters, with the Prime Minister able to appear instead for three hours. ETHI minutes record the witnesses' absence, that the committee had not released them, and that Ministers Pablo Rodriguez and Mona Fortier appeared after ordering the staff not to appear. What stopped it: The staff did not appear; ministers appeared in their place after instructing them not to attend. The committee recorded their non-attendance.
