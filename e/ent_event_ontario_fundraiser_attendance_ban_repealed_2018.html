Ontario ban on MPPs attending fundraisers repealed (Bill 57, 2018)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Bill 57 repealed s. 23.1 of the Election Finances Act, which had barred MPPs, ministers, party leaders and some staff from attending political fundraising events, effective Jan 1, 2019 (e-Laws; Cassels). It is the same statute that closed the three watchdog offices already in this layer.
