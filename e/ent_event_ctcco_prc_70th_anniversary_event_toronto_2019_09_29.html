CTCCO event for the 70th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China, Toronto area (Sept 29, 2019), attended by the PRC consul general

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Epoch Times reports that the Confederation of Toronto Chinese Canadian Organizations (CTCCO), whose president is Weng Guoning, organized an event in the Toronto area on Sept 29, 2019 to mark the PRC's 70th anniversary, and that PRC Consul General Han Tao attended. It names as attendees federal Small Business Minister Mary Ng, Liberal MP Geng Tan, Ontario PC MPPs Vincent Ke and Aris Babikian (who presented congratulatory proclamations), and city councillors Jim Karygiannis (Toronto) and Isa Lee (Markham). It also says MPP Billy Pang sent a representative.
