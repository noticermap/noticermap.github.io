Written question Q-3278 (foreign-interference inquiry notices) unanswered at prorogation (2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Q-3278 (Richard Bragdon, Dec 9, 2024) asked whether any minister, current or former exempt staff member or public servant had received a notice from the Commissioner of the Public Inquiry into Foreign Interference under section 13 of the Inquiries Act of an intended misconduct or adverse finding, and details of each. It remained 'Awaiting response' when the session ended Jan 6, 2025; the page states: 'The government is no longer required to respond to this question.' What stopped it: Prorogation ended the session before an answer was tabled.
