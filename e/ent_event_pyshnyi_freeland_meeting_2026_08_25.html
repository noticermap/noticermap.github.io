Meeting between NBU Governor Andriy Pyshnyi and Chrystia Freeland (2026-08-25)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Interfax-Ukraine reported on 2026-08-25 that NBU Governor Andriy Pyshnyi met Chrystia Freeland, Adviser to the President of Ukraine on Economic Development, and quoted his post that they discussed strengthening dialogue with the international financial community and foreign business to turn interest in Ukraine into investment decisions. The same report quotes him saying the NBU is ready for direct dialogue with foreign business and would continue that work with Freeland. This is a contemporaneous news report of his account, not an NBU minutes publication.
