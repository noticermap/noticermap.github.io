Jewish Seniors Alliance of British Columbia Society

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Seniors' peer-support and advocacy society with an active BC Lobbyists Registry in-house registration (10896-5785, since 2024-05-03; senior officer Jeff Moss, Executive Director; 35 lobbying activity reports). Registered topics centre on barrier-free home support for BC seniors (elimination of co-payments) and its Vancouver Coastal Health funding contract.
