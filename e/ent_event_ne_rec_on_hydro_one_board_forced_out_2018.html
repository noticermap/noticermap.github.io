Hydro One's entire board resigns and CEO Mayo Schmidt retires under agreement with the new Ontario government; Hydro One Accountability Act passed (July-Aug 2018)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On July 11, 2018 Energy Minister Greg Rickford accepted a letter of agreement from Hydro One chair David Denison. The entire board would resign and be replaced on or before August 15, 2018, CEO Mayo Schmidt would retire that day with a $400,000 lump sum and no severance, and CFO Paul Dobson would be acting CEO (Ontario Newsroom). The Hydro One Accountability Act, 2018 then required a new executive compensation framework subject to provincial approval. The board's terms were cut short through the province's shareholder leverage (it held a minority stake) after a PC campaign pledge. Stopped (as reported): Board resignations and replacement by Aug 15, 2018; CEO retirement; statute imposing compensation controls (by: Minister of Energy Greg Rickford (Government of Ontario), by agreement with the board).
