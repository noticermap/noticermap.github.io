Alberta PAC DynaLife AHS audit meetings refused (2025-12-02)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Opposition member Mr. Schmidt moved a spring 2026 meeting schedule that would include audit-focused meetings on the Auditor General's report 'An Examination of Community Laboratory Services (Contract with DynaLife)' and the Auditor General's upcoming report on Alberta Health Services procurement practices. The motion was defeated 3-6 (for: Ellingson, Eremenko, Schmidt; against: Lunty, van Dijken, Sawyer, Rowswell, Wiebe, Lovely); a second Schmidt motion for two audit-focused meetings was also defeated. In debate Mr. Schmidt quoted remarks he attributed to the Premier on AHS procurement. What stopped it: Recorded votes 3-6; Chair announced 'This motion is defeated.'
