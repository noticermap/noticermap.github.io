Person

Edmund de Rothschild (1916-2009)

Partner 1947, senior partner 1960, chairman of N M Rothschild & Sons Ltd 1970-75; Brinco director

Public records ✓Verified in public records

From 1952 led the consortium that developed Hamilton (Churchill) Falls, Labrador; crossed the Atlantic 'more than 400 times' over the 20-year project (archive). Director of Brinco. His papers (largely closed) include Churchill Falls, BRINCO and Straflo files and two letters from Winston Churchill about Brinco shares Churchill bought (1956, 1957).
