Suicide Action Montréal benefit evening "Tombent les masques", $175,000 raised, Montreal (Feb 2018)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Le Journal de Montréal's society column (Rodger Brulotte, Feb 12, 2018) reported that Suicide Action Montréal's benefit evening, under the honorary chairmanship of Québecor CEO Pierre Karl Péladeau, raised $175,000, with partners Scotiabank, Vidéotron, STM and Langlois avocats; photo captions place Mayor Valérie Plante at the event.
