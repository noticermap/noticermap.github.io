House order to produce Winnipeg lab documents not complied with (2021)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The House of Commons ordered production of unredacted documents on the firing of National Microbiology Laboratory scientists Xiangguo Qiu and Keding Cheng; PHAC president Iain Stewart was admonished at the bar by Speaker Anthony Rota on June 21, 2021 for not producing them (CBC). The government went to Federal Court to block release, then dropped the case after Parliament was dissolved in Aug 2021, ending the order (CBC).
