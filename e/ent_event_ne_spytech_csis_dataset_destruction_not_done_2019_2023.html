CSIS kept dataset information the CSIS Act required destroyed from July 2019 (NSIRA review 21-15)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The CSIS Act dataset regime (from the National Security Act, 2017; in force July 2019) requires datasets not meeting the regime to be destroyed. NSIRA found that 'as of August 2023, CSIS did not comply with the dataset provisions in the CSIS Act' because it retained Canadian information extracted from foreign datasets that 'should have been destroyed upon coming into force of the NSA 2017, in July, 2019', and that CSIS had not exhaustively scanned its systems. What stopped it: Per NSIRA, CSIS retained the information and had not exhaustively scanned its systems as of Aug 2023. CSIS's response says the records were kept 'to meet a corporate record keeping obligation' and later destroyed, and rejects the exhaustive scan as 'not feasible' (response PDF pp.4-5).
