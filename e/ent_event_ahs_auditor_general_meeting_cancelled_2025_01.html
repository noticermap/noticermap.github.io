AHS meeting with the Auditor General on procurement review cancelled (alleged, Jan 2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In her wrongful-dismissal lawsuit, former AHS CEO Athana Mentzelopoulos alleges that after she was fired on Jan 8, 2025, Andre Tremblay, who replaced her as interim CEO, ordered staff to cancel a meeting that week with the Auditor General about AHS's forensic audit and internal procurement review (CBC). These are unproven allegations. The province called the interpretation that her termination was due to the review "false" (CBC). Auditor General Doug Wylie opened his own examination on Feb 6, 2025.
