Online Harms Act (Bill C-63) passage in the 44th Parliament

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Bill C-63, sponsored by Justice Minister Arif Virani and introduced Feb 26, 2024, remained at second reading when the session ended on Jan 6, 2025 (LEGISinfo), so it died on the Order Paper. Wiretap reported in 2026 that the government was reintroducing it.
