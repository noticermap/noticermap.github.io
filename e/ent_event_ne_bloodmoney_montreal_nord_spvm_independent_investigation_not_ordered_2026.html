Quebec named an observer instead of ordering an independent investigation of alleged racism by Montréal-Nord SPVM officers

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Two Station 39 officers were suspended and 16 investigated over alleged racist and hateful acts. Community groups called for an independent investigation. Minister Ian Lafrenière left the SPVM in charge of its own investigation and appointed lawyer Anne-Marie Boisvert as an independent observer, saying a public inquiry was not ruled out after the criminal investigation. Stopped (as reported): Minister appointed observer Anne-Marie Boisvert (June 20, 2026). The SPVM keeps both the criminal and the disciplinary probes (by: Quebec Minister of Domestic Security Ian Lafrenière (observer appointed instead; BEI not assigned)).
