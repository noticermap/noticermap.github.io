Bal des lumières mental-health gala, $1.43M raised, Montreal (Mar 23, 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

A Bell Canada news release says the second Bal des lumières, held at the Bell Centre on Mar 23, 2016, raised $1,430,000 for three Montreal mental-health foundations, with more than 850 guests including Health Minister Gaétan Barrette, Transport Minister Jacques Daoust and Montreal Mayor Denis Coderre, who is quoted. The release says the ball was co-chaired by George Cope (Bell), Serge Godin (CGI), Geoff Molson (Club de hockey Canadien/Bell Centre/evenko) and Louis Vachon (National Bank).
