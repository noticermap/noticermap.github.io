Alberta Assembly sets aside the independent Electoral Boundaries Commission's final map and hands redistribution to an MLA committee (Government Motion 37, Apr 2026)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Electoral Boundaries Commission, chaired by retired judge Dallas Miller, tabled an 89-seat final report on March 26, 2026, with a majority report, an addendum by the chair recommending 91 seats, and a minority report. Government House Leader Joseph Schow's Government Motion 37 urged a bill for 91 divisions and set up a Select Special Committee on Electoral Boundaries, chaired by Brandon Lunty, to oversee an advisory panel. A time-allocation motion (GM 39) passed 46-35, and GM 37 passed 44-36 on April 21, 2026 (Votes and Proceedings). CBC reported the motion rejects the commission's proposed boundaries and that the new MLA committee would not hold public hearings on a new map. Stopped (as reported): Government Motion 37 agreed 44-36 after time allocation (GM 39, 46-35); redistribution moved to a 5-member MLA committee and a 91-seat process (by: Legislative Assembly (government majority)).
