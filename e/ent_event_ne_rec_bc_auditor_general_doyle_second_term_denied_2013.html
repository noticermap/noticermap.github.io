BC legislative committee declines to give Auditor General John Doyle a second term, advertises the post, then under pressure offers only a two-year extension (Jan 2013)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Auditor General John Doyle had criticized the BC Liberal government on issues including access to Basi-Virk documents and BC Hydro deferral accounts. He sought a second six-year term. Reappointment needed a unanimous recommendation from the five-member special committee (3 Liberal, 2 NDP). In early January 2013 the committee advertised for a new auditor general, which signalled the reappointment had been refused. NDP caucus chair Shane Simpson blamed the Liberal members. On January 23, 2013, after Premier Christy Clark publicly asked MLAs to reconsider, the committee unanimously offered Doyle a two-year extension instead of a full term (Times Colonist). Clark said legislation would move future auditors general to a single eight-year term. Stopped (as reported): Committee refused reappointment and began an external search; it then offered a two-year extension only (by: Special Committee to Appoint an Auditor General (BC Legislative Assembly; Liberal majority)).
