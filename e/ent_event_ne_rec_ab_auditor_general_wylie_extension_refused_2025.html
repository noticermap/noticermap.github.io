Alberta Auditor General Doug Wylie's request for a two-year extension to finish the health-procurement examination not granted; committee votes 5-4 to search for a successor (Nov 2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Auditor General Doug Wylie, whose eight-year term ended April 28, 2026, asked the Standing Committee on Legislative Offices for a two-year extension so he could complete and report his examination of procurement at Alberta Health and AHS (CBC, Edmonton Journal). On Nov 3, 2025 UCP MLA Scott Cyr moved that the committee recommend a special committee to search for a new Auditor General; it carried 5-4 on a recorded vote (transcript). At the same meeting the committee also voted 5-4 to defer the Auditor General's supplementary funding request to December. Cyr said Wylie could reapply. The committee later recommended Phillip D. Peters, effective April 29, 2026 (committee report). Stopped (as reported): Committee motion (Cyr) to recommend a special search committee for a new Auditor General carried 5-4; Assembly passed Government Motion 13 on Nov 25, 2025; Phillip D. Peters recommended March 25, 2026 (by: Standing Committee on Legislative Offices (UCP majority)).
