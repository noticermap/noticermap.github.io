Citizen recall petition against Calgary Mayor Jyoti Gondek declared insufficient; recall did not proceed

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Under Alberta's 2022 recall provisions in the Municipal Government Act, Landon Johnston filed a notice to recall Mayor Jyoti Gondek on Jan. 30, 2024 and had Feb. 5 to April 4, 2024 to collect 514,284 signatures (40% of the 2019 population). On May 13, 2024 City Clerk Kate Martin declared the petition insufficient: 69,344 unverified petitioners, and zero valid signatures in a 369-signature random sample because the notice of recall was missing from the petition pages (MGA s. 240.7(3)(c)). Stopped (as reported): City Clerk declared the petition insufficient at a special council meeting on May 13, 2024 (by: Calgary City Clerk Kate Martin (statutory threshold under MGA Part 7.1 not met)).
