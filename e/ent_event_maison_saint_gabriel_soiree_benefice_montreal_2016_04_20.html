Maison Saint-Gabriel benefit evening "Du grain à la table", about $285,000 raised, Montreal (Apr 20, 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Info-Culture reported that the Maison Saint-Gabriel's 2016 soirée-bénéfice on Apr 20, 2016 drew about 550 people and raised close to $285,000; speakers included Quebec Economy Minister Dominique Anglade and Mayor Denis Coderre, described as "un fidèle" of the event. Borough mayors Benoit Dorais and Jean-François Parenteau also attended.
