Ports public inquiry recommended never called

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Kenny committee recommended in 2002 and again in 2003 (Rec. 4.2) a public inquiry under the Inquiries Act into crime and terrorism vulnerabilities at Canada's ports. Transport Canada's July 7, 2006 response did not address it, and no such federal inquiry was held. What stopped it: The government response quoted in Seaports (Transport Canada, July 7, 2006) lists ISPS/MTSR, waterside security and MTSCP measures but, per the Committee, 'makes absolutely no reference to the recommendation, which calls for a public inquiry under the Inquiries Act'.
