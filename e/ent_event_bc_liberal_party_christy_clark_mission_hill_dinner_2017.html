BC Liberal $5,000-a-plate fundraiser dinner with Premier Christy Clark, Mission Hill Winery, West Kelowna (Jan 26, 2017)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Premier Christy Clark attended a BC Liberal fundraiser dinner at Mission Hill Winery on Jan 26, 2017 at $5,000 per plate; Global News reported 20 guests and the Globe reported 12 contributing guests per the BC Liberals. Clark defended the event the next morning at a Greater Westside Board of Trade breakfast. Kelowna Capital News reported it was organized by Agriculture Minister Norm Letnick.
