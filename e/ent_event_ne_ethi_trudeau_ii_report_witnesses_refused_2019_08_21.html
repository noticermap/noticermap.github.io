ETHI trudeau II report witnesses refused (2019-08-21)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

After the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner's Trudeau II Report, ETHI met in August 2019. Peter Kent's motion to invite the Commissioner to brief the Committee on his report was negatived 4-5; Charlie Angus's motion to invite Prime Minister Justin Trudeau, Finance Minister Bill Morneau, adviser Ben Chin and the Clerk of the Privy Council (to explain not sharing Cabinet confidences with the Commissioner, who felt his investigation was hampered) was negatived 3-6; and Lisa Raitt's motion requesting author Aaron Wherry's interview materials was negatived 2-7. What stopped it: Three motions negatived on recorded divisions: Kent 4-5 (NAYS Baylis, Fortier, MacKinnon, McCrimmon, Vandenbeld); Angus 3-6; Raitt 2-7. Committee adjourned to the call of the Chair.
