ETHI motion for speaking-fee records filibustered (Oct 2020)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

A Conservative motion, backed by the NDP and Bloc, to order Speakers' Spotlight records of fees paid since 2008 to Justin, Sophie Grégoire, Margaret and Alexandre Trudeau was filibustered by Liberal members; after four and a half hours the Oct 9, 2020 meeting adjourned without a vote when Bloc MP Marie-Hélène Gaudreau voted with the Liberals (National Post). The first attempt had been blocked by the Aug 18 prorogation (NP).
