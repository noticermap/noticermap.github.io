Québec Forte et Fière fundraising cocktail with Mayor Bruno Marchand, Quebec City (Sept 12, 2025): about 475 people

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Le Carrefour de Québec reports that Mayor Bruno Marchand and his party Québec Forte et Fière held their fundraising cocktail on Friday, Sept 12, 2025, a week before the campaign began, with about 475 donors, volunteers and supporters; Marchand spoke. The party said it had $150,000 in donations as of Sept 12. Le Journal de Québec had reported $140,830 from 1,006 donors the day before.
