Saskatchewan Public Accounts Committee defeats NDP motion asking the Provincial Auditor to investigate the Global Transportation Hub's attempt to land a Costco store (May 13, 2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Costco, which had planned a Regina store at Westerra, had been in talks about locating at the provincial Global Transportation Hub (GTH). Regina council then approved an incentive package of almost $7 million to keep it at Westerra (620 CKRM). On May 13, 2025 NDP MLA Hugh Gordon moved that the Public Accounts Committee, under s. 16(1) of The Provincial Auditor Act, ask the Provincial Auditor to perform a special assignment investigating the GTH's actions, procedures, policies and expenditures in the attempted deal. The motion was defeated on a voice vote; the Chair declared that 'the nos have it' (PAC Hansard). GTH minister David Marit did not take media questions, and GTH CEO Daniel Hersche said Costco's agent had approached GTH's broker (620 CKRM). Stopped (as reported): Motion defeated (voice vote) (by: Government (Saskatchewan Party) members of the Public Accounts Committee).
