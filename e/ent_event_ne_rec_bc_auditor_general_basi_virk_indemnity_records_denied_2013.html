BC Supreme Court refuses the Auditor General access to privileged records behind the $6M Basi-Virk legal-fee indemnity, limiting his audit (Jan 2013)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In October 2010, former ministerial aides Dave Basi and Bobby Virk pleaded guilty in the BC Rail corruption case, and the government announced it had paid about $6 million in their legal fees. The Opposition demanded a public inquiry, which was not held. Auditor General John Doyle asked the court for the documents. Justice Robert Bauman dismissed the application, finding it a sweeping invasion of solicitor-client privilege (Times Colonist, Jan 30 2013). The OAG's December 2013 audit of special indemnities states that solicitor-client privilege prevents the Office from auditing the legal bills, and asks for that barrier to be addressed. Stopped (as reported): Court dismissed the AG's application; privilege bars audit of the legal bills (by: BC Supreme Court (Bauman J.), on solicitor-client privilege grounds).
