Toronto integrity commissioner suspended a complaint probe into Mayor John Tory's Rogers-related vote because of the 2022 election freeze

Public records ◔Listed in public records

A complaint alleged Mayor John Tory should not have voted on an ActiveTO road-closure item affecting the Rogers-owned Blue Jays. Integrity Commissioner Jonathan Batty said he could not finish before the election-period cut-off and suspended the inquiry. Stopped (as reported): Batty said he could not complete the inquiry before the Aug. 19, 2022 cut-off after which no code-of-conduct report can go to council during the election period; file suspended (by: Integrity Commissioner Jonathan Batty, applying the election-period reporting freeze).
