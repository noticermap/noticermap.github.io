Event · British Columbia

BC Liberal majority on the Finance committee votes 5-4 to refer the 2012 Health Ministry firings to the Ombudsperson rather than call the public inquiry sought by the fired researchers and the NDP (July 2015)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In 2012 the Ministry of Health fired several drug-research employees and announced an RCMP referral. The researchers and the Opposition NDP called for a public inquiry. On July 29, 2015 the all-party committee instead voted 5-4, on party lines, to refer the matter to Ombudsperson Jay Chalke, after legislation was amended to strengthen his powers. Chalke had asked for a unanimous approach (CBC). The Ombudsperson's 2017 report, Misfire, found the investigation flawed and the dismissals unjustified, and government accepted its recommendations. Stopped (as reported): Committee referral to the Ombudsperson passed 5-4; no public inquiry called (by: Select Standing Committee on Finance and Government Services (Liberal majority)).
