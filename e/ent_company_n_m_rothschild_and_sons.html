N M Rothschild & Sons

Public records ✓Verified in public records

London bank founded by Nathan Mayer Rothschild, who settled at New Court as a banker by 1809 (archive timeline). Today N. M. Rothschild & Sons Limited (Companies House 00925279, incorporated 1968), whose person with significant control since 13 May 2020 is Rothschild & Co Continuation Limited (75%+ of shares and votes). In the 19th century its main business was issuing bonds to raise loans, 'largely for sovereign governments' (archive).

Also known as: NMR, N. M. Rothschild & Sons Limited, New Court
