Montreal council rejected an opposition motion asking the auditor general to probe Quebec gun-violence funds for the SPVM

Public records ◔Listed in public records

After the Journal de Montréal reported that Quebec questioned how Montreal used provincial money meant for the SPVM, Ensemble Montréal asked council to have the vérificatrice générale investigate. Council rejected the motion on Tuesday, Sept. 20, 2022 (Journal Métro). Stopped (as reported): Motion rejected by the Projet Montréal majority. Executive committee chair Dominique Ollivier called the insinuations 'graves, inacceptables et fausses' (by: Montreal council majority (Projet Montréal)).
