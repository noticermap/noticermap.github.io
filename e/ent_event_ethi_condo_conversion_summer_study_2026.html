ETHI summer study of the Canada–B.C. condo conversion plan (proposed July 2026)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

At ETHI meeting 49, Conservative MP Aaron Gunn moved an urgent summer study of at least six meetings with witnesses including Gregor Robertson, Bob Rennie and Brookfield; Liberal MP Fares Al Soud moved to adjourn debate, agreed 5–4 (yeas: Al Soud, Bardish Chagger, Wade Chang, Claude Guay, Linda Lapointe; nays: Gunn, Gabriel Hardy, Jacob Mantle, Luc Thériault), per the minutes. The Globe and Mail reported Liberal MPs called the study premature.
