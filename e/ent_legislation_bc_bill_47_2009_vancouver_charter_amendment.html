Vancouver Charter Amendment Act, 2009 (Bill 47)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

BC bill passed at a special sitting (third reading Jan 17, 2009). It added s.190.1 to the Vancouver Charter, letting the City borrow, lend, guarantee, take security and take an assignment of a loan to finance the Southeast False Creek (Olympic Village) project.
