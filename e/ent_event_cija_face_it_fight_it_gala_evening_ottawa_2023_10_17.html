CIJA 'Antisemitism: Face It, Fight It' conference gala evening with the PM and party leaders; lifetime achievement award to Irwin Cotler, Ottawa (Oct 17, 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

iPolitics/The Canadian Press reports that the Centre for Israel and Jewish Affairs (CIJA) organized the 'Antisemitism: Face It, Fight It' conference in Ottawa, and that Prime Minister Justin Trudeau, Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre, NDP Leader Jagmeet Singh and Bloc Leader Yves-François Blanchet spoke on Oct 17, 2023. The CJN says CIJA national chair Gail Adelson-Marcovitz welcomed Trudeau to the podium and that the evening ended with thanks to Irwin Cotler. The Jewish Independent reports that Cotler received a CIJA lifetime achievement award on stage with Trudeau, Poilievre and Blanchet, and that Justice Minister Arif Virani also gave a speech.
