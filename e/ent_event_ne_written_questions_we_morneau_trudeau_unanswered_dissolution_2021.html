Written questions on WE, Morneau and Trudeau (Q-778, Q-791) unanswered at dissolution (2021)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Q-778 (Gerald Soroka, May 28, 2021) asked about the emails concerning WE Charity sent by the former Finance Minister's office to Ontario ministers' offices, cited in paragraph 50 of the Morneau II Report. Q-791 (Rob Moore, June 3, 2021) asked whether the Department of Justice provided legal services under the Policy on Legal Assistance and Indemnification in relation to the Ethics Commissioner's Trudeau III and Morneau II Reports, for people including the Prime Minister, Bill Morneau, Ian Shugart and Katie Telford. Both remained 'Awaiting response' when the 43-2 session ended Aug 15, 2021, and both pages state: 'The government is no longer required to respond to this question.' What stopped it: The session ended (Aug 15, 2021) before answers were tabled.
