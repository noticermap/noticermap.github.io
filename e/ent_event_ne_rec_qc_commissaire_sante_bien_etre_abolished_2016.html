Quebec's 2016-17 budget ends the independent Commissaire à la santé et au bien-être (health system performance watchdog); the post sits inactive until revived in 2019

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Commissaire à la santé et au bien-être (CSBE) was created in 2005-06 to independently evaluate the performance of Quebec's health and social services system. Its commissioner since 2006 was Robert Salois. The office's 2016-17 annual report records that the cessation of its activities was announced on March 16, 2016, in the 2016-17 budget. Salois said he learned of it from a civil servant without explanation (Radio-Canada/CP). Health Minister Gaétan Barrette said its functions overlapped with other bodies and that the move made economic sense (CBC). In March 2019 the CAQ government's Health Minister Danielle McCann said the post, then 'inactive', would be re-established (Radio-Canada). Stopped (as reported): Budget 2016-17 announced cessation of the CSBE's activities; most staff departed (by: Government of Quebec (Couillard, Liberal); Minister of Health Gaétan Barrette).
