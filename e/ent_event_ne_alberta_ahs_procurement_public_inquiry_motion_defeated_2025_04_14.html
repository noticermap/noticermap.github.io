Alberta AHS procurement public inquiry motion defeated (2025-04-14)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Motion Other Than Government Motion 521, moved by Sarah Hoffman, urged the government to commence a public inquiry under section 2 of the Public Inquiries Act into recent allegations of improper procurement contracts entered into by Alberta Health Services, including chartered surgical facilities, with a judge as commissioner and section 7 powers. It was defeated on division 29-45; those voting for included Guthrie and Sinclair alongside Opposition members. What stopped it: Defeated on recorded division, 29 for, 45 against.
