Sir Evelyn de Rothschild

Chairman of N M Rothschild & Sons Ltd 1976-2003

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Chairman of NMR from 1976 and of Rothschilds Continuation Holdings AG from 1982, until the 2003 merger of the French and UK houses; director of De Beers 1977-94 (archive). Attended the 1998 ROM gala preview in Toronto. Cross-reference: Epstein layer covers his spouse Lynn Forester de Rothschild (work/research/epstein).
