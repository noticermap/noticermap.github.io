Digital charter C-27 ended prorogation (2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Government Bill C-27, An Act to enact the Consumer Privacy Protection Act, the Personal Information and Data Protection Tribunal Act and the Artificial Intelligence and Data Act ... (Digital Charter Implementation Act, 2022), sponsored by the Minister of Innovation, Science and Industry, was introduced in the 44th Parliament, 1st session. LEGISinfo records its final status for that session (November 22, 2021 to January 6, 2025) as 'At consideration in committee in the House of Commons' (latest activity: Second reading and referral to committee on Monday, April 24, 2023); it did not reach Royal Assent. The session ended with the Proclamation Proroguing Parliament to March 24, 2025 (SI/2025-9, January 6, 2025). What stopped it: Prorogation of Parliament on January 6, 2025 ended the session with the bill still unfinished.
