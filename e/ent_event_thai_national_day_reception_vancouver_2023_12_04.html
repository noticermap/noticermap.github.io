Royal Thai Consulate-General National Day reception, Vancouver (Dec 4, 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Royal Thai Consulate-General's own page says it held a reception on Dec 4, 2023 for Thailand's National Day, attended by BC Forests Minister Bruce Ralston (minister responsible for the consular corps) as guest of honour, BC MLAs, the Mayor of Vancouver and city councillors. Mayor Ken Sim's public calendar lists 'Thai National Day Reception' that day.
