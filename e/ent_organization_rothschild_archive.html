The Rothschild Archive (London)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Archive of N M Rothschild & Sons and the Rothschild family, run by The Rothschild Archive Trust; public website rothschildarchive.org with a catalogue ('Guide to the Archive'; 946 pages captured by the Internet Archive per the reel creator's index), research guides, family-tree pages and Annual Reviews (1995-2014). On 2026-11-26 the site returned HTTP 503 'Down for Maintenance'; its last Internet Archive capture before the outage is 2026-10-11. Its 1999 bulletin described it as 'an integral part of the Rothschild worldwide group of companies'.

Also known as: rothschildarchive.org, The Rothschild Archive Trust
