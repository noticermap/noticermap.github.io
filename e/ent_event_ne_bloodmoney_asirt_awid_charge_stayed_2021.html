Crown stayed ASIRT's assault charge against Edmonton Const. Dylan Awid without giving a public reason

Public records ◔Listed in public records

ASIRT charged Edmonton Police Service Const. Dylan Awid with assault in October 2021 over force allegedly used after a handcuffed arrest on June 11, 2019. The Crown stayed the charge on Nov. 8, 2021, and neither the ministry nor ACPS gave a reason by deadline (Edmonton Journal). Stopped (as reported): Charge stayed Nov. 8, 2021. The ministry said only that ACPS's prosecution standard is higher than the police standard (by: Alberta Crown Prosecution Service (stay)).
