Alberta Office of the Election Commissioner dissolved (Bill 22, Nov 2019)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Bill 22, the Reform of Agencies, Boards and Commissions and Government Enterprises Act, ended Election Commissioner Lorne Gibson's contract (to run to 2023) and moved the role into the Office of the Chief Electoral Officer; Elections Alberta said the office was dissolved Nov 22, 2019. Gibson had levied more than $200,000 in fines in his investigation of the 2017 UCP leadership race (CBC, Global). The Bill was introduced by Finance Minister Travis Toews; the Opposition called it "corrupt" (Edmonton Journal).
