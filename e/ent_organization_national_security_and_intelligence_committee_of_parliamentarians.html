National Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians

Wiretap Public records ✓Verified in public records

Wiretap Committee of parliamentarians that reviews national security and intelligence activities.

Public records Statutory committee of 11 MPs and senators (Act: Royal Assent 2017-06-22) that reviews federal national security and intelligence activity with top-secret access; reports go to the PM, who may order injurious material removed before tabling. Chairs: David McGuinty (2017-2024), Patricia Lattanzio (from 2025-01-29), Darren Fisher (from 2025-09-18).

Also known as: NSICOP, CPSNR
