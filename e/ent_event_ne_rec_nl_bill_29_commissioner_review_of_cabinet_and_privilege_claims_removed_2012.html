Newfoundland and Labrador Bill 29 removes the Information and Privacy Commissioner's power to review refusals based on official Cabinet records and solicitor-client privilege (June 2012)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Bill 29, An Act to Amend the Access to Information and Protection of Privacy Act, was introduced on June 11, 2012 by Justice Minister Felix Collins after the Cummings statutory review. It broadened the definition of Cabinet records (NL release). The OIPC's 2012-13 annual report states that Bill 29 removed the Commissioner's authority to review refusals based on solicitor-client privilege (s. 21) or official Cabinet record claims (s. 18(2)(a)), and that he could no longer demand those records; applicants had to go to the Trial Division instead. The bill passed after a filibuster of about 70 hours (June 11-14) and came into force June 27, 2012 (OIPC). The 2014-15 independent ATIPPA review led to a replacement Act in 2015. Stopped (as reported): Bill 29 amendments in force (by: Government of Newfoundland and Labrador (PC); House of Assembly).
