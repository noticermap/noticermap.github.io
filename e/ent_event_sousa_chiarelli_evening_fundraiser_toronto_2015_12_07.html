'An evening with Hon. Bob Chiarelli, MPP and Hon. Charles Sousa, MPP' – $7,500-a-ticket Ontario Liberal fundraiser, Toronto (Dec 7, 2015)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Ontario Finance Minister Charles Sousa and Energy Minister Bob Chiarelli attended an invitation-only Ontario Liberal fundraiser one month after the Hydro One IPO. Per the Integrity Commissioner's report, 24 people from financial institutions, unions, government-relations firms and the energy sector paid $7,500 each; nine of the 16 banks involved in the Hydro One offerings had representatives there; the honorary chair was John Sherrington of Scotiabank. The Globe and Mail reported the event was a two-hour cocktail reception and dinner, and that the Liberals' Elections Ontario filing listed only a $4,000 event that day. Commissioner J. David Wake found no contravention of s.6 of the Members' Integrity Act but called for a review of the Act.
