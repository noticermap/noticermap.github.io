Integrity commissioner probe of ex-councillor Justin Di Ciano was suspended for a police referral, then terminated after OPP charges were withdrawn

Public records ◔Listed in public records

A 2016 code-of-conduct complaint about then-councillor Justin Di Ciano was suspended in 2017 when the integrity commissioner referred matters to police. OPP charges under the Municipal Elections Act laid in 2018 were withdrawn in 2020, and the integrity commissioner then terminated the inquiry. Stopped (as reported): Suspended Aug. 11, 2017 on referral to police; OPP charged Di Ciano Nov. 16, 2018; charges withdrawn Feb. 24, 2020; inquiry terminated in a December 2020 report to council (by: Integrity Commissioners Valerie Jepson (suspension, 2017) and Jonathan Batty (termination, 2020); Crown withdrawal of OPP charges).
