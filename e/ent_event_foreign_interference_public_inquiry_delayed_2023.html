Public inquiry into foreign interference: recommended against, then called (2023)

Wiretap Public records

Independent Special Rapporteur David Johnston, appointed by Prime Minister Justin Trudeau, recommended on May 23, 2023 against a public inquiry; CBC reported the government had said it would follow his recommendation. After all recognized parties agreed, the Hogue inquiry was established by P.C. 2023-0882 on Sept 7, 2023, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister (per the order). Minister Dominic LeBlanc announced it the same day (Government of Canada news release).
