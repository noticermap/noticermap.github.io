Liberal $500 private reception with Justice Minister Jody Wilson-Raybould at Torys LLP offices, Toronto (Apr 7, 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

A $500-a-ticket Liberal Party 'private reception' with Justice Minister and Attorney General Jody Wilson-Raybould was held at the Toronto offices of Torys LLP, hosted personally by partner Mitch Frazer. CTV, citing Elections Canada records, reported 20 people, mostly lawyers, attended and the event raised $9,577. Wilson-Raybould said she attended as an MP. Ethics Commissioner Mary Dawson declined to investigate (per CTV).
