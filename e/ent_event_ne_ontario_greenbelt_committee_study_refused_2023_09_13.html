Ontario greenbelt committee study refused (2023-09-13)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Mary-Margaret McMahon moved under Standing Order 113(a) that the Committee study the government's decision to remove lands from the Greenbelt, with public hearings September 27-October 3 and report writing October 5, inviting the former Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing, associate and deputy ministers, the Provincial Land and Development Facilitator and potentially staff of the Premier's office and Cabinet Office. The motion was lost on a recorded vote (Ayes: Andrew, McMahon, Stevens; Nays: Babikian, Grewal, McGregor, Pang, Sabawy, Thanigasalam). What stopped it: Recorded vote: 3 ayes, 6 nays; Chair declared the motion lost.
