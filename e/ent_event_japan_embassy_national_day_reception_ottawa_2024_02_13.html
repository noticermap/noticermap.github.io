Embassy of Japan National Day reception (Emperor's birthday), Ottawa (Feb 13, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Embassy of Japan says Ambassador Kanji Yamanouchi hosted about 700 guests on Feb 13, 2024 for the Emperor's birthday national day reception, including House Speaker Greg Fergus, Ministers Lawrence MacAulay, François-Philippe Champagne, Ahmed Hussen and Ya'ara Saks, and MP Terry Sheehan. Fergus and Champagne gave congratulatory remarks and Sheehan proposed the toast; Japanese companies had booths.
