£4,000,000 advance to the British Government for Suez Canal shares (1875)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

N M Rothschild & Sons advanced Prime Minister Disraeli, acting for the British Government, £4,000,000 to buy the Khedive of Egypt's Suez Canal Company shares; repaid within five months (archive).
