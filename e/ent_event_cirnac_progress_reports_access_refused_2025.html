CIRNAC access request for Tk'emlúps program progress reports deemed refused (2025-26)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Information Commissioner found CIRNAC missed its extended June 23, 2025 deadline on a request for "Activity Progress Reports" on the Tk'emlúps Indian Residential School Survivor Project and was "deemed to have refused access"; 576 pages had been retrieved but review had not begun. She ordered a complete response within 36 business days (Jan 13, 2026). Todayville reported Blacklock's later received 23 heavily redacted pages.
