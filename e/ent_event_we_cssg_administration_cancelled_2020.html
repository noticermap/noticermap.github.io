WE Charity administration of the Canada Student Service Grant cancelled (July 2020)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

WE Charity's contribution agreement to administer the Canada Student Service Grant was terminated by mutual agreement on July 3, 2020, and the program returned to the federal government (CBC; WE Charity's own statement). Minister Bardish Chagger called it "a mutually agreed upon decision" (CBC). The Procurement Ombudsman and the ETHI committee later reviewed the contract.
