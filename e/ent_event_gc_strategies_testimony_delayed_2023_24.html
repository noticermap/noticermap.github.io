GC Strategies partners' committee testimony on ArriveCAN delayed until House order (2023–24)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

OGGO reported that GC Strategies partners Kristian Firth and Darren Anthony refused to testify after summonses issued Nov 2, 2023 and Feb 9, 2024; the House then ordered both to appear within 21 days. Firth appeared on March 13, 2024, and Speaker Greg Fergus said on March 22 that both had now appeared (OGGO reports 14 and 17; openparliament).
