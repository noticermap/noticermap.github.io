$500 Liberal fundraiser featuring Finance Minister Bill Morneau, tickets sold with help of Apotex chairman Barry Sherman, Toronto (Nov 7, 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Globe reported Apotex chairman Barry Sherman helped organize and sell $500 tickets for a Nov 7, 2016 Liberal fundraiser featuring Finance Minister Bill Morneau at the home of philanthropist Nancy Pencer and funeral home executive Michael Benjamin, while Apotex was registered to lobby Finance Canada. The lobbying commissioner later concluded no investigation was necessary for this event.
