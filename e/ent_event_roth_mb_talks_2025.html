Manitoba officials' talks with Rothschild & Co advisers on an AI data centre and the Conawapa dam (Oct-Nov 2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Per emails, meeting notes and a pitch document obtained by CBC: Manitoba's top bureaucrat and three deputy ministers met Rothschild & Co advisers in Oct-Nov 2025; Rothschild & Co offered to help fund an $18-billion hydro dam and proposed an NDA then an RFP. Premier Kinew said he was not aware the province ever formally engaged Rothschild & Co.
