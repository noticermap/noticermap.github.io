Canada 2020 'A Time to Build: Economic Lookahead Dinner' with Mark Carney, Toronto (Apr 22, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Canada 2020, a think tank, held a sold-out Economic Lookahead Dinner in Toronto on April 22, 2024 with a keynote by Mark Carney, then Chair of Canada 2020's Advisory Board (not then in elected office; later Liberal leader and Prime Minister). CPAC aired the keynote and a conversation with Jennifer Sloan of Mastercard Canada. Wiretap posts describe it as the 'Canada 2020 Lockheed dinner'; the event page does not name sponsors.
