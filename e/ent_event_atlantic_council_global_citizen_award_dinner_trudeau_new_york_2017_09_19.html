Atlantic Council Global Citizen Awards dinner honouring PM Justin Trudeau, New York (Sept 19, 2017)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Atlantic Council's release says it honoured Prime Minister Justin Trudeau at its eighth annual Global Citizen Awards dinner in New York on Sept. 19, 2017, before an audience of around 500 government, business and civil-society leaders; his award was presented by Queen Rania Al Abdullah of Jordan. Other honorees: President Moon Jae-in and pianist Lang Lang (presented by Klaus Schwab). The Council published Trudeau's remarks.
