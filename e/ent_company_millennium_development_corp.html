Millennium Development Corp.

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Vancouver developer whose 2006 bid won the Olympic Village market parcels; the project company MSEFC sat under it. MDC and its principals guaranteed the project's obligations, and MDC pledged its MSEFC shares to the lender (KPMG report to the City, 2009).
