PACP never voted on John Nater motion for ArriveCAN subcontractor lists (Feb 15, 2024)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

At PACP meeting 100, John Nater moved a production motion. It would have ordered CBSA, PHAC, PSPC and other departments, the main ArriveCAN contractors (GC Strategies, Dalian Enterprises Incorporated, Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Canada, TEKsystems, Donna Cona, BDO Canada LLP, MGIS, 49 Solutions, Makwa Resourcing/TPG Technology Consulting, Advanced Chippewa Technologies) and the Auditor General to provide complete lists of ArriveCAN subcontractors or "Other" contractors, the services provided and the amounts paid, within seven days. It also ordered GC Strategies and departments to list all GC Strategies subcontractors since 2015. The minutes record "Debate arose thereon." and then "At 5:18 p.m., the committee adjourned to the call of the Chair." No vote is recorded. The evidence shows the bells were ringing; the Chair asked for unanimous consent to continue and members said no, and the meeting was adjourned. The motion does not appear in the minutes of PACP meetings 101 to 108 (Feb 20 to Mar 19, 2024). What stopped it: "Debate arose thereon." followed by "At 5:18 p.m., the committee adjourned to the call of the Chair." The evidence at block (1715) records the Chair: "The bells are ringing. Is there UC to continue to the bottom of the hour?"; "Some hon. members: No."; then "The meeting is adjourned." No recorded vote on the motion.
