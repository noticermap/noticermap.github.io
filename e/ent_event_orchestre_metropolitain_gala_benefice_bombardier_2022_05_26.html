Orchestre Métropolitain 6th benefit gala at a Bombardier plant, $505,000 raised, Dorval (May 26, 2022)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Orchestre Métropolitain's own page says its 6th benefit gala on May 26, 2022, held at Bombardier's Laurent Beaudoin Completion Centre in Dorval under the honorary chairmanship of Bombardier CEO Éric Martel and presented by TD Bank Group, raised $505,000; it lists Montreal Mayor Valérie Plante and former premier Pauline Marois among guests.
