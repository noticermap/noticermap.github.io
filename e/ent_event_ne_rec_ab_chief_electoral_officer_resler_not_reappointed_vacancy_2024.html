Alberta Chief Electoral Officer Glen Resler not reappointed; office vacant and run by acting officers May-Nov 2024

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On Nov 7, 2023 the Legislative Offices committee recommended a search for a new Chief Electoral Officer rather than reappointing Glen Resler, whose contract ran to May 2024 (transcript; the Globe reported the motion carried without opposition). The committee's June 2024 report says 'the position of Chief Electoral Officer is currently vacant'; with the deputy resigning, it recommended LaRae Petrowsky as Acting Chief Electoral Officer from July 4, 2024. The search committee recommended Gordon McClure in October 2024. Stopped (as reported): Office vacant after May 2024; acting appointment recommended June 20, 2024; permanent successor recommended October 2024 (by: Standing Committee on Legislative Offices; Select Special Ethics Commissioner and Chief Electoral Officer Search Committee).
