CSIS warrant application held 54 days in the Public Safety minister's office (2021)

Public records Sam Cooper / The Bureau ◔Listed in public records

Public records The foreign interference inquiry heard that a CSIS warrant application sat in then-Public Safety Minister Bill Blair's office for 54 days after it went to his chief of staff Zita Astravas; Blair testified he signed it the day he first saw it (CBC). The Bureau reported the warrant concerned former Ontario minister Michael Chan and that Democracy Watch would file a conflict complaint.

Sam Cooper / The Bureau The Bureau reports that a CSIS warrant concerning Liberal powerbroker Michael Chan sat in Public Safety Minister Bill Blair's office for 54 days in 2021; Blair's former chief of staff Zita Astravas denied delaying it at the Hogue Commission (per The Bureau).
