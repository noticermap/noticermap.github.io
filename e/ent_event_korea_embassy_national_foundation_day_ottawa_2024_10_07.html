Embassy of the Republic of Korea National Foundation Day event co-hosted with Senator Yonah Martin, Ottawa (Oct 7, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Korean Embassy says it and Senator Yonah Martin co-hosted an Oct 7, 2024 event for National Foundation Day and the new Korean Heritage Month, with about 450 guests, including Senate Speaker Raymonde Gagné, House Speaker Greg Fergus, Ministers François-Philippe Champagne and Ahmed Hussen, and Parliamentary Secretary Rob Oliphant.
