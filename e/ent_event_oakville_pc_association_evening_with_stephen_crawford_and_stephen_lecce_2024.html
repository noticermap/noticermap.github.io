Oakville PC Association $500-a-plate fundraiser with MPP Stephen Crawford and Minister Stephen Lecce, Oakville (Nov 5, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Oakville News reported that Oakville Mayor Rob Burton left a council meeting on Tuesday (Nov 5, 2024) to attend a $500-per-plate fundraiser for the Oakville Progressive Conservative Association, billed as an evening with local member Stephen Crawford (the article calls him 'Oakville MP') and special guest Stephen Lecce, Ontario Minister of Energy and Electrification. Burton confirmed his attendance, said he did not pay, and said he went to thank Lecce in person.
