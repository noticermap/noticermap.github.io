Embassy of Israel 'Celebration of Israel at 75' / Second Canadian Summit, Ottawa (mid-May 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Ambassador Ronen Hoffman wrote that the Embassy's 'flagship event of the year: A Celebration of Israel at 75!' would include the Second Annual Canadian Summit, with partners the Jewish National Fund and Israel Bonds and support from the Canada-Israel Interparliamentary Group. Critic Yves Engler's blog says the summit took place the week before May 22, 2023, co-sponsored with CIJA, and names Minister Karina Gould and MPs Pierre Paul-Hus, Melissa Lantsman, Randall Garrison, Anthony Housefather and Ya'ara Saks as participants (the MPs on a panel).
