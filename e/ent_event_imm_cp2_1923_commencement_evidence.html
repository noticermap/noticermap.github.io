1923 commencement mechanism remains unresolved

Immigration

Original c.38 gives June 30 assent and no explicit commencement clause. R.S.C.1906 Interpretation Act s.7(2) makes assent the default unless later commencement is provided. This creates a question, not proof overturning the official-history July 1 date: intervening Interpretation amendments and Gazette/implementation instruments require checking.
