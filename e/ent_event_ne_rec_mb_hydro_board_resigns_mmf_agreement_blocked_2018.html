Nine of ten Manitoba Hydro-Electric Board members resign after the Premier blocks a Hydro-Manitoba Métis Federation agreement and declines to meet the board (Mar 21, 2018)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On March 21, 2018 chair H. Sanford Riley and eight directors wrote to Crown Services Minister Cliff Cullen and resigned effective immediately. They said that for over a year they had tried to meet the Premier on critical finance and governance issues, including Hydro's relationship with Indigenous communities (Manitoba Hydro statement). Premier Brian Pallister said the trigger was the government's refusal to approve a payment of about $70 million to the Manitoba Métis Federation connected to the Manitoba-Minnesota transmission line, which he called 'persuasion money'. Riley called that characterization 'cynical, offensive and wrong' and said the agreement had been carefully vetted (CBC). Only PC MLA Cliff Graydon remained on the board. Stopped (as reported): Government declined to approve the MMF payment; board resigned en masse (by: Premier Brian Pallister / Government of Manitoba).
