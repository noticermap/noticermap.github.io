Liberal $1,500 private fundraiser with PM Justin Trudeau at Benson Wong's home, Toronto (May 19, 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Globe and Mail reported that Prime Minister Justin Trudeau was the top draw at a $1,500 Liberal Party fundraiser at the Toronto home of Benson Wong, chair of the Chinese Business Chamber of Canada, attended by 32 other people. Guests included Zhang Bin (a political adviser to the Chinese government) who, with Niu Gensheng, pledged $1 million to the Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation and the University of Montreal law faculty weeks later; the Globe found no evidence Zhang paid to attend. Also present per the Globe: Shenglin Xian (founder of Wealth One Bank, then awaiting regulatory approval), Bob Guo and Liu Meng. The Tyee reported attendees paid a reported $1,525. A later Globe report said invitees were asked for $4,500-$5,000, above the contribution limit.
