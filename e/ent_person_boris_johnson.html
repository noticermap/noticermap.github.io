Boris Johnson

Former Prime Minister of the United Kingdom (2019–2022); former Mayor of London (2008–2016)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Named in the @CovenantCanada video as a 'schoolmate' of Ghislaine Maxwell who had a Davos meeting with Mark Carney before Carney's Bank of England job. Rachel Johnson wrote in 2021 that Boris Johnson and Maxwell were contemporaries at Balliol College, Oxford, in the 1980s (The Independent; The Telegraph, 2021-11-17); 'schoolmate' overstates university contemporaneity. The Evening Standard (2013-08-02) reported that, as Mayor of London, Johnson first met Carney during an hour-long meeting at Davos (date not given) and met him in London on 2013-08-01. Johnson was not the official who appointed the Bank of England governor.
