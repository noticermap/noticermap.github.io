Équipe Denis Coderre fundraising cocktail, $300 a ticket, Montreal (June 3, 2015)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

La Presse reported that elected members of Équipe Denis Coderre were each asked to sell up to 20 tickets ($6,000) at $300 apiece for the party's June 3, 2015 fundraising cocktail in Montreal, while the party carried a debt (about $450,000 per party spokesperson Françoise Stanton). Le Journal de Montréal reported Coderre saying ticket buyers would get no special treatment ("Pas de passe-droit").
