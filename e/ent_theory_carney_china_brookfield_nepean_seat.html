Carney's China ties, Brookfield's New York move and the Nepean seat (Hillier)

Theories (unverified) ◔Named in an unverified theory

In KbT_kbyUHm8 (2026-02-12; V-MCcp-6rP0 is a clip) he argues Carney is 'intentionally disrupting trade with the United States' to win a majority, citing the Beijing visit, the Reagan ad and a self-computed '98%' Bayesian estimate.
