BC Supreme Court ruling on Olympic Village buyers' discontinued misrepresentation claims (Moon v. Sails, 2012 BCSC 1999)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

In five buyer suits over 69 Olympic Village units (REDMA rescission claims against the City and the building sales companies), the buyers had dropped their alternative misrepresentation claims. The court held they needed no leave to do so and that this was not an abuse of process, but ordered that the discontinuance bars any later suit on the same claims. It made no finding on the alleged deficiencies.
