Ontario PC Leader's Dinner with Premier Doug Ford, $1,500 a ticket, closed to media (Mar 2, 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Toronto Star reported Ontario PCs raised a record $6 million at a sold-out, media-closed fundraiser on Thursday (Mar 2, 2023) at the Toronto Congress Centre in Etobicoke, where more than 4,000 people paid $1,500 each. Attendees, speaking confidentially, described elected officials, lobbyists, developers, union leaders, political staffers and lawyers. Guests Ford named in his speech: former premier Mike Harris, Brampton Mayor Patrick Brown, Markham Mayor Frank Scarpitti, Oakville Mayor Rob Burton, Peel Region chair Nando Iannicca and York Region chair Wayne Emmerson. Ford did not mention the Greenbelt.
