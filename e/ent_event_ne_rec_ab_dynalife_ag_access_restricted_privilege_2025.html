Auditor General's DynaLIFE examination limited in scope: AHS, backed by the Health department, claimed legal and Cabinet privilege, redacted documents and destroyed the ex-CEO's notebooks (Nov 2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Auditor General Act gives the Auditor General access to information (s.14) and the power to compel evidence under oath (s.14.1). In 'An Examination of Community Laboratory Services (Contract with DynaLIFE)', released Nov 19, 2025, the Auditor General reported that AHS restricted access, 'supported by the Department of Health'. AHS claimed legal and Cabinet privilege over a wide range of documents, sometimes without clear rationale, ran a line-by-line lawyer review of more than 10,000 documents, and redacted large sections of thousands of them. Records were missing, password-locked or destroyed, including the former CEO's notebooks, 'despite our request to preserve evidence'. The office declared a scope limitation of indeterminate effect. Global reported 1,200 documents were fully redacted. Stopped (as reported): Privilege claims, redactions and destroyed/missing records caused a declared scope limitation (by: Alberta Health Services, supported by the Department of Health (per the Auditor General)).
