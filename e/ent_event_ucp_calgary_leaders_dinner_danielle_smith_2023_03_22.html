UCP Calgary Leader's Dinner with Premier Danielle Smith, $250 a ticket (Mar 22, 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Calgary Herald reported Premier Danielle Smith spoke to a sold-out crowd of more than 900 at the $250-per-ticket UCP Leader's Dinner at a Calgary hotel on March 22, 2023, the first Leader's Dinner for a sitting Tory government in Alberta since 2015 (Jason Kenney had declined to continue the events). She reiterated support for a new Flames arena and an airport CTrain link. A 2024 UCP advisory (per the Herald) put the 2023 attendance at 950.
