Alberta Health Minister Fred Horne fires the entire AHS board after it declined to cancel executive pay-at-risk bonuses; Janet Davidson named official administrator (June 2013)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On June 12, 2013 Health Minister Fred Horne dismissed the whole Alberta Health Services board, including chair Stephen Lockwood. The day before, the board had declined a government directive to reconsider 2012-13 executive pay-at-risk payments. Janet Davidson was appointed official administrator to act in place of the board and review the bonus decision (CBC, Globe). Lockwood called it political interference (CBC). The board structure was restored in 2015 (Edmonton Journal, 2022). Stopped (as reported): Entire board dismissed without severance; official administrator appointed (by: Minister of Health Fred Horne (PC government)).
