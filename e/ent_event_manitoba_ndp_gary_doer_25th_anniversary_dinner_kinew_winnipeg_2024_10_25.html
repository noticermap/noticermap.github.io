Manitoba NDP dinner marking 25th anniversary of Gary Doer government, with Premier Wab Kinew (Winnipeg, Oct 25, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Manitoba NDP's own event page advertised a dinner on Oct 25, 2024 in Winnipeg celebrating the 25th anniversary of Gary Doer's 1999 election, with special guest speaker NDP leader Wab Kinew, and says the dinner sold out. Tiers listed: Golden Boy Sponsor $3,000 (table for 8 plus VIP reception), Silver Anniversary Sponsor $2,500, Broadway Sponsor $1,500, Patron $500, Host $250, Guest $150. Organizer's own claim.
