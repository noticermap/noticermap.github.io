Alberta Crown declined ASIRT's recommendation to charge a second RCMP officer over a 2020 Maskwacis arrest

Public records ◔Listed in public records

ASIRT found several instances of assault by two RCMP officers during a Nov. 14, 2020 arrest in Maskwacis, which was largely captured on in-car video. Alberta Crown Prosecution Service said charges against Subject Officer #2 would not meet the reasonable-likelihood-of-conviction threshold; charges against Subject Officer #1 proceeded. Stopped (as reported): ACPS declined to prosecute the second officer; ASIRT accepted the opinion and laid no charge (by: Alberta Crown Prosecution Service).
