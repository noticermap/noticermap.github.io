Surrey Mayor's Evening of Giving hosted by Mayor Brenda Locke (Oct 4, 2024): over $650,000 raised

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The City of Surrey says the Oct 4, 2024 Mayor's Evening of Giving, hosted by Mayor Brenda Locke and presented by Peter Wall Financial Corporation, raised more than $650,000 for the Surrey Fire Fighters' Charitable Society, over 20% above 2023. A LinkedIn snippet (not opened) gives a final cheque of $705,258.
