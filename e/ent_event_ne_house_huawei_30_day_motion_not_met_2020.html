House motion for a Huawei 5G decision within 30 days not met (2020)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The House agreed (Division No. 23, 178-146) to Michael Chong's motion, seconded by Pierre Paul-Hus, calling on the government to decide on Huawei's involvement in Canada's 5G network within 30 days and to table, within 30 days, a plan to combat China's foreign operations and intimidation. ISED's policy statement announcing the intent to prohibit Huawei and ZTE in 5G networks is dated May 19, 2022. What stopped it: The Huawei decision was announced on May 19, 2022 (about 18 months later). This record set does not establish whether or when the plan in part (b) was tabled.
