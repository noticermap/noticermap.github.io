Doug Ford and Sylvia Jones did not testify at the Emergencies Act inquiry (Nov 2022)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The POEC summoned Premier Doug Ford and Sylvia Jones on Oct 24, 2022 to testify Nov 10. They asserted parliamentary privilege; Justice Fothergill held the summonses valid but ruled they could resist them while the Ontario legislature sat (2022 FC 1513). Archive record: The two invoked parliamentary privilege; the Federal Court declared they may resist the summonses and the Commission cannot enforce attendance.
