Liberal Laurier Club Stampede reception with PM Mark Carney, Calgary (July 5, 2025)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Globe and Mail's Politics Insider reported that Prime Minister Mark Carney's Stampede itinerary for Saturday July 5, 2025 included a party fundraising event, the Laurier Club Stampede reception, accompanied by ministers Eleanor Olszewski (Emergency Management), Rechie Valdez (Women and Gender Equality) and Tim Hodgson (Energy). The Hill Times also reported Carney would appear at the Laurier Club Stampede reception on July 5. Ticket price not stated in these sources.
