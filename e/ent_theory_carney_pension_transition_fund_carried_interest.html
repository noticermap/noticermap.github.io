Carney used public pensions and the Canada Growth Fund to enrich Brookfield and himself (Hillier)

Theories (unverified) ◔Named in an unverified theory

Hillier says Carney, while an unpaid adviser outside the Conflict of Interest Act, used his influence to bring PSP and Ontario Teachers' into Brookfield's transition fund as anchors, then used the Canada Growth Fund to de-risk Brookfield's Entropy stake, front-loading carried interest so that he 'becomes a billionaire'; he calls Trudeau and Freeland complicit. In 8j78WxZ7KL4 and hmyyaYb5v9g he adds that GFANZ 'collapsed' under antitrust scrutiny and that Carney turned to Canadian pensions instead.
