Manitoba never launched a search for a permanent IIU civilian director after Roxanne Gagné resigned in October 2024

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Freedom-of-information records obtained by the Investigative Journalism Foundation show 'no competition' and 'no records' of a hiring process for a permanent director of the Independent Investigation Unit between Jan. 1, 2024 and July 7, 2026. Bruce Sychuk has been acting director since November 2024. Stopped (as reported): No competition held as of July 7, 2026 (FOI response). The acting designation stays in force until a civilian director revokes it (by: Government of Manitoba (no competition launched)).
