Michael Bailey (Bovale Developments)

Epstein ✓Verified in public records

Irish developer. He is the seller's contact on the 2012 helicopter sale to Hyperion Air. Status: No criminal conviction located. The Flood Tribunal's Second Interim Report (2002) found he arranged the June 1989 meeting at which a £30,000 payment to Ray Burke was made, found the payment corrupt and found he obstructed the tribunal. These are tribunal findings, not a court verdict.
