de Rothschild Frères (Paris)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Paris banking house founded by James Mayer de Rothschild (from 1812). 'Funded many loans to European governments' and built railways (archive). Renamed Banque Rothschild in 1967; nationalised by the French government in 1981 (became Compagnie Européenne de Banque); its records are at the Archives nationales du monde du travail, Roubaix (fonds 132 AQ).

Also known as: Banque Rothschild, MM. de Rothschild Frères
