Ontario closes three independent legislative watchdog offices (Nov 2018)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Ontario's Fall Economic Statement (Nov 15, 2018), delivered by Finance Minister Vic Fedeli, eliminated the Environmental Commissioner, the Provincial Advocate for Children and Youth and the French Language Services Commissioner as standalone offices, folding their mandates into the Auditor General and the Ombudsman (CBC). Child advocate Irwin Elman said he learned of it through the media (CBC).
