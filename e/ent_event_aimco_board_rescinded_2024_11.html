AIMCo board of directors removed and replaced (Nov 2024)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

By O.C. 298/2024 (Nov 7, 2024) the Alberta cabinet rescinded the appointments of all 10 AIMCo directors and appointed Finance Minister Nate Horner a member and designated him chair of the board (O.C. 298/2024; CBC: the board's "sole director and chair"); by O.C. 318/2024 (Nov 20, 2024) it rescinded Horner and appointed Stephen Harper chair, reappointing three former directors (per the King's Printer orders). CBC reported CEO Evan Siddall and three other executives were also out.
