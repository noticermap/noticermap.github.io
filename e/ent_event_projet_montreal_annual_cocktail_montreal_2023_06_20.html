Projet Montréal annual fundraising cocktail with Mayor Valérie Plante, Montreal (June 20, 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Projet Montréal's own post says more than 700 people financially supported the party at its annual fundraising cocktail on June 20, 2023, where Mayor Valérie Plante spoke. La Presse reported the online invitation sold $100 tickets and advertised the chance to meet Plante and the party's elected officials; Plante defended it as following the rules.
