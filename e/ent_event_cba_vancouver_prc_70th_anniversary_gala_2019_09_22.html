Chinese Benevolent Association of Vancouver (CBA) gala for the 70th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China, Vancouver (Sept 22, 2019)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The National Post reports that the Chinese Benevolent Association of Vancouver (CBA) organized a gala in Vancouver on Sept 22, 2019 to mark the 70th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China, and that PRC Consul General Tong Xiaoling was on stage. It reports that Defence Minister Harjit Sajjan appeared and stood on stage with the consul general, and that BC ministers Bruce Ralston and George Chow attended. Sajjan's spokesman said he attended as a candidate, spoke briefly, raised the two detained Canadians, and left before dinner. The Epoch Times says online photos also show Senator Yonah Martin. The CBA's website, as cited by the National Post, puts attendance at nearly 1,000.
