Theory (unverified)

Klaus Schwab, 2017: 'half of this cabinet, or even more than half of this cabinet, are actually Young Global Leaders'

Theories (unverified) ◔Named in an unverified theory

On 20 September 2017, at the Harvard Kennedy School Institute of Politics (John F. Kennedy Jr. Forum; 2017 Malcolm H. Wiener Lecture on International Political Economy, “Strengthening Collaboration in a Fractured World”), World Economic Forum founder and executive chairman Klaus Schwab, in conversation with David Gergen, said of Justin Trudeau’s cabinet that he would know “that half of this cabinet, or even more than half of this cabinet, are actually Young Global Leaders of the World Economic Forum,” and that “we penetrate the cabinets.” The remarks are on the official Institute of Politics recording (18:04). The numerical claim is unverified. A complete public YGL or alumni directory was not found; WEF class lists opened in this pass do not support “half” or “more than half,” and do not disprove it. Unverified theory — reported claims only; no wrongdoing alleged. No causation is stated or implied.
