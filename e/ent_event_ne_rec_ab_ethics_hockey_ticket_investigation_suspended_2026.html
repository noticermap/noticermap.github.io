Alberta Ethics Commissioner suspends investigation into MLAs accepting NHL hockey tickets under s.25(6) (active law-enforcement investigation) (Oct 2026)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Speaker Ric McIver wrote on Oct 14, 2026 asking about the status of the Ethics Commissioner's investigation into MLAs accepting hockey tickets, noting s.24(6) bars the Assembly from inquiring into a matter referred to the Commissioner. In an Oct 20 reply, Ethics Commissioner Shawn McLeod said the investigation referenced in Feb 4, 2025 correspondence was suspended under s.25(6) (Edmonton Journal). The Commissioner's 2026 bulletin says s.25(6) requires suspension when the subject matter is also under law-enforcement investigation, and the investigation may resume once that is disposed of. The Journal said it was unclear whether this was linked to the RCMP's health-procurement investigation. Stopped (as reported): Investigation suspended under s.25(6) of the Conflicts of Interest Act (by: Ethics Commissioner of Alberta (statutory suspension)).
