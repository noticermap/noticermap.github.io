Chinese Immigration Act repealed

Immigration

Original c.19 assented May14,1947; s.4 expressly repeals the Chinese Immigration Act, R.S.C.1927 c.95. Sections1–3 also amend transit bonds, repeal s.80 and legislate service-dependant admission. Assent and repeal provision are verified; no separate commencement proclamation found or assumed.
