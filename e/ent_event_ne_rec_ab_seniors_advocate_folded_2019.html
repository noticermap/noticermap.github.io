Alberta Office of the Seniors Advocate eliminated and consolidated into the Office of the Alberta Health Advocates (Budget 2019, effective Jan 2, 2020)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Office of the Seniors Advocate resolved individual seniors' issues and made systemic recommendations to the Minister. The Seniors and Housing 2019-20 Annual Report says that 'as part of Budget 2019, the OSA was consolidated with the Office of the Alberta Health Advocates under the Ministry of Health', which took over its responsibilities on Jan 2, 2020. Reporting said Seniors Advocate Dr. Sheree Kwong See's appointment ended Dec 31, 2019. Critics said non-health issues such as housing and income lost a dedicated advocate (Edmonton Journal; National Association of Federal Retirees). Stopped (as reported): OSA consolidated into the Health Advocate under Budget 2019; Health Advocates assumed its responsibilities Jan 2, 2020 (by: Government of Alberta (UCP), Minister of Seniors and Housing Josephine Pon).
