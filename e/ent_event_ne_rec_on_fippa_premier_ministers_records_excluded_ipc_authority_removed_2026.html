Ontario Bill 97 retroactively removes records of the Premier, ministers and their staff from FOI law, taking the Premier's personal-device use outside the Privacy Commissioner's authority (Apr 2026)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Opposition Leader Marit Stiles asked the Information and Privacy Commissioner to investigate the Premier's use of personal devices for government business. On April 27, 2026 the IPC replied that once the Bill 97 amendments (Plan to Protect Ontario Act (Budget Measures), 2026) come into force, records held by the Premier, cabinet ministers, parliamentary assistants and their staff are excluded from FIPPA, retroactive to 1988, and fall outside the IPC's statutory authority to investigate (IPC). CBC/Canadian Press reported that Premier Doug Ford said part of the rationale was to stop a Global News request for his cellphone records. The government skipped committee hearings, and the bill passed 57-33 on April 23, 2026. Stopped (as reported): Bill 97 enacted; exclusion retroactive to January 1, 1988; IPC states the records are outside its authority to investigate (by: Government of Ontario (PC majority), Legislative Assembly of Ontario).
