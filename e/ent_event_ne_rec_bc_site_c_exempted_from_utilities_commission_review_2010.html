Clean Energy Act exempts the Site C dam and other BC Hydro projects from BC Utilities Commission certificate review (2010)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Under the Utilities Commission Act, major utility projects need a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity from the BCUC (ss. 45-47), and energy supply contracts are reviewed under s. 71. Bill 17, the Clean Energy Act, was introduced in April 2010 by Energy Minister Blair Lekstrom. Section 7(1) exempts BC Hydro from those sections for listed projects, including (d) Site C, the third Peace River dam, and s. 7(3) bars the commission from acting to prevent the projects. NDP energy critic John Horgan said the exempted projects would not have appropriate oversight (CBC). The 2017 BCUC inquiry into Site C was an advisory review requested by government, not a certificate proceeding. Stopped (as reported): Statutory exemption, Clean Energy Act s. 7(1)(d) and s. 7(3) (by: BC Liberal government (Minister of Energy, Mines and Petroleum Resources), Legislative Assembly).
