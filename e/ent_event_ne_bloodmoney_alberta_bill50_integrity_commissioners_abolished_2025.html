Alberta's Bill 50 repealed municipal codes of conduct, ending Calgary's integrity commissioner complaint process (Edmonton affected too)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Municipal Affairs Minister Ric McIver introduced Bill 50 on April 8, 2025 to eliminate mandatory municipal codes of conduct. The City of Calgary says its Code of Conduct Bylaw 26M2018 was repealed in May 2025 and its Integrity Commissioner no longer takes complaints. Stopped (as reported): Bill 50 introduced April 8, 2025; Calgary's bylaw repealed May 2025; complaints no longer accepted (by: Government of Alberta (Municipal Affairs Minister Ric McIver), Bill 50).
