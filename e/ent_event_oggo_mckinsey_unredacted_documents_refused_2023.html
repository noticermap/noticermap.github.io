OGGO McKinsey production order: deadline missed and unredacted documents refused (2023)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

OGGO's Jan 18, 2023 motion required McKinsey contract documents from 20 departments and agencies by Feb 22, 2023. ESDC filed 125 pages by the deadline and 8,102 by April 6; on March 10, 2023 its deputy minister "responded to the OGGO request for the documents in unredacted format by denying the request" (ESDC binder). CBSA resubmitted about 4,000 pages on June 2, 2023 with some redactions remaining for third parties and cabinet confidence (CBSA).
