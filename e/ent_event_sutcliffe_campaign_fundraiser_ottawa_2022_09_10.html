$1,200 fundraiser for mayoral candidate Mark Sutcliffe during a Redblacks game, Ottawa (Sept 10, 2022)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Ottawa Citizen reports that on Saturday, Sept 10, 2022, mayoral candidate Mark Sutcliffe attended a campaign fundraiser in Ottawa whose event page listed a $1,200 'attendance fee' (the municipal maximum), organized by Daniel Fernandes, Peter Hudson and Jeff Hunt. Horizon Ottawa called it a cash-for-access event; Sutcliffe called that framing 'bogus' and said the amount was a suggestion.
