Montreal decided not to equip SPVM officers with body cameras after a year-long pilot

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The SPVM ran a body-camera pilot with 78 officers and reported on Jan. 29, 2019 that full rollout would cost $17.4M over five years plus $24M a year. Mayor Valérie Plante announced on Feb. 6, 2019 that the city would not go ahead. Stopped (as reported): Plante announced at the executive committee that the city would not proceed, citing cost. Axon said the estimate was inflated (by: City of Montreal administration (Mayor Valérie Plante)).
