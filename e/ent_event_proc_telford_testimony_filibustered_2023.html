PROC testimony by Katie Telford delayed by Liberal filibuster (Mar-Apr 2023)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Liberal PROC members filibustered for about 24 hours from early March 2023 "in an attempt to prevent Telford from testifying" (National Post). The PMO announced March 21 she would appear; Liberals withdrew from the speakers list, and she testified April 14, 2023 (CPAC). Archive record: Meeting adjourned (6-5) on Julian's motion on March 1; motion to resume consideration negatived 5-6 on March 2 (YEAS Berthold, Brock, Cooper, Gray, Normandin; NAYS Fergus, Fragiskatos, Julian, Romanado, Sahota, Turnbull).
