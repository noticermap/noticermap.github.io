B.C. Bill 15 time-allocation motion: leave refused (May 28, 2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Rob Botterell sought leave to move that section 11 of the time allocation order adopted May 6, 2025 for Bill 15 (Infrastructure Projects Act) be discharged. Hansard records 'Leave not granted.' Section 11 of the May 6 order required all remaining stages of Bill 15 to be disposed of by 8 p.m. on May 28, 2025. What stopped it: Leave to move the motion was not granted, so it was never debated.
