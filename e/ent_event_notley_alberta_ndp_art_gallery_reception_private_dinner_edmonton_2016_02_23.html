Alberta NDP fundraiser at the Art Gallery of Alberta: $250 reception and cancelled $1,000 private dinner with Premier Notley, Edmonton (Feb 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Alberta NDP scheduled a two-part fundraiser at the Art Gallery of Alberta: a reception at $250 a ticket, and a by-invitation $1,000-a-ticket private dinner with Premier Rachel Notley and NDP MLAs in a separate room. The private dinner was cancelled (postponed) hours before it was due to begin after Ethics Commissioner Marguerite Trussler opened an investigation on a Wildrose complaint; the reception went ahead. Trussler later cleared Notley.
