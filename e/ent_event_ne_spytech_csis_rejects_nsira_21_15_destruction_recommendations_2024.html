CSIS rejects NSIRA 21-15 recommendations to destroy retained information and scan its systems (2024)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

NSIRA review 21-15 recommended that CSIS immediately destroy names retained from exigent-circumstances queries (R2), immediately destroy a case study dataset collected under s.12 (R12), and conduct an exhaustive scan of its repositories to destroy non-compliant information (R8). The government response marks all three 'Disagree'. On R2 and R12 CSIS states 'NSIRA's interpretation of 'strictly necessary' is overly narrow such that it would unreasonably impede CSIS' ability to meet its mandates' (pp.2, 6); on R8 it says an exhaustive search 'is not feasible' (p.5). CSIS also disagreed with R6 (cease duplicating information). What stopped it: CSIS disagreed with Recommendations 2, 8 and 12 in the published government response.
