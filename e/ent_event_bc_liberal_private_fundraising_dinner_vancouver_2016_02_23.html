Private BC Liberal fundraising dinner with Premier Christy Clark and Minister Rich Coleman, Vancouver (Feb 23, 2016): over $1 million raised

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

theBreaker reports that a private BC Liberal fundraising dinner coordinated by Patrick Kinsella was held in Vancouver on Feb 23, 2016, with Premier Christy Clark and Housing Minister and Deputy Premier Rich Coleman as special guests, raising more than $1 million. It links the dinner to Feb 26, 2016 donations, including $200,000 and $100,000 from Peter Wall companies, $100,000 from Bruno Wall's company, and $200,000 from John Redekop Construction plus $100,000 from Peter Redekop.
