BC NDP $700 invitation-only breakfast with Housing Minister Ravi Kahlon for candidate Ravi Parmar, Langford (May 17, 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Black Press reported a $700 invitation-only breakfast fundraiser on May 17, 2023 featuring B.C. Housing Minister Ravi Kahlon in support of NDP by-election candidate Ravi Parmar (Langford-Juan de Fuca). An email advertising it bore the signature of Keycorp Developments, and promised attendees would hear about the Homes for People plan; cheques were to be payable to 'BC NDP'. BC Green House Leader Adam Olsen called it 'a cash-for-access exclusive event designed for developers'. The NDP said ticketed fundraisers are legal and invitations went to a wide range of people.
