FINA WE documents redacted (2020)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Pierre Poilievre's motion before FINA proposed reporting to the House that the Committee's July 7, 2020 order for WE Charity / Me to We contract and CSSG documents (redactions to be made by the Law Clerk) had not been complied with, quoting the Law Clerk that departments had redacted on grounds not contemplated in the order and that his Office had not seen the unredacted documents; it also noted that the August 18, 2020 prorogation prevented consideration. An amendment and a Gerretsen subamendment (suspending debate until testimony on the redactions) were debated across meetings from October 15 to November 19, 2020, with the meeting of October 28 running, with suspensions, to November 4, and meeting 5 running to November 19, when an 11-0 compromise set a November 24 deadline for unredacted documents (except ATIA s.69) to the Law Clerk and appearances by the Clerk of the Privy Council and the Ethics Commissioner. What stopped it: Departments redacted on grounds the Law Clerk said were not contemplated by the order; the motion to report non-compliance to the House was then held up for weeks of debate and suspensions (meetings 2, 4 and 5) before being replaced by a negotiated compromise.
