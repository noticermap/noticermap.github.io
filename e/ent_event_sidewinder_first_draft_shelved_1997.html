Project Sidewinder first draft shelved and project suspended (1997)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

SIRC's 1999 review (SIRC 1999-10) says the joint CSIS-RCMP Project Sidewinder study was "suspended temporarily in June 1997 and restarted in early 1998", that CSIS shelved the first draft, disposed of "transitory documents" and could not locate others it said were "misfiled". SIRC found "no evidence of political interference". The Bureau (2025) reports the author's allegation that the report was suppressed and documents destroyed, which SIRC contradicts.
