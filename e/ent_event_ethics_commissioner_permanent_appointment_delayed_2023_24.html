Permanent federal Ethics Commissioner appointment delayed about a year (2023-24)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

After Mario Dion left in early 2023 the office was run by interim commissioners, Konrad von Finckenstein from Aug 30, 2023 (CIEC annual report). CBC reported in Feb 2024 that the government was running out of time to name a permanent commissioner; the PMO announced von Finckenstein's seven-year appointment Feb 29, 2024. Democracy Watch criticized the process.
