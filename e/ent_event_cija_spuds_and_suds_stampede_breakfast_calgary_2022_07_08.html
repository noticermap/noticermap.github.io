CIJA / Calgary Jewish Federation 'Spuds and Suds' Stampede Breakfast with Premier Kenney, Opposition Leader Notley and Mayor Gondek, Calgary (July 8, 2022)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Alberta Jewish News reports that the Spuds and Suds Stampede Breakfast on July 8, 2022, a collaboration of CIJA, CJPAC, Calgary Jewish Federation and Jewish Federation of Edmonton (first year as sponsor), brought together politicians and staffers. Attendees included Israel's Ambassador Ronen Hoffman, Premier Jason Kenney, NDP Leader Rachel Notley, Mayor Jyoti Gondek, federal and provincial Conservative leadership candidates, city councillors, MLAs and MPs. Photos are credited to Calgary Jewish Federation.
