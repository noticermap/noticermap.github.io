Written questions on SDTC (Q-3232 and others) unanswered at prorogation (2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Four written questions on Sustainable Development Technology Canada asked in Nov 2024 still showed 'Awaiting response' when the 44-1 session ended on Jan 6, 2025: Q-3232 (Rick Perkins, Nov 29, 2024: redactions and pages in the government's response to the June 10, 2024 SDTC production order), Q-3218 (John Nater, Nov 26: SDTC Independent Ethics Advisor), Q-3181 (John Brassard, Nov 18: SDTC board costs since 2016) and Q-3178 (Brassard, Nov 18: SDTC 2022 Leadership Summit costs). Each page states: 'The government is no longer required to respond to this question.' What stopped it: Prorogation ended the session before answers were tabled; the House site marks each question as no longer requiring a response.
