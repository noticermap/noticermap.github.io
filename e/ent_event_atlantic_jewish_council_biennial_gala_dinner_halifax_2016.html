Atlantic Jewish Council 20th biennial conference gala dinner, Halifax (autumn 2016), with Minister Labi Kousoulis and Opposition Leader Jamie Baillie

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Atlantic Jewish Council's own Shalom magazine (Winter 2016-17) reports that a gala Shabbat dinner with more than 200 delegates opened the AJC's 20th biennial conference in Halifax and honoured retiring executive director Jon Goldberg. Greetings came from former Lieutenant Governor Myra Freeman, Nova Scotia minister Labi Kousoulis, Opposition Leader Jamie Baillie, and officials from Jewish Federations of Canada and CIJA.
