Event

BC Court of Appeal dismisses MSEFC property transfer tax appeal (2011 BCCA 206)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

The Court of Appeal dismissed MSEFC's appeal from 2010 BCSC 1156 on the preliminary objection alone: MSEFC had to follow the statutory assessment and appeal process rather than seek an advance court declaration about a specific, unusual transaction. It did not decide the tax question itself.
