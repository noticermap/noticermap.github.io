ETHI never voted on the Hepfner motion on RCMP use of Pegasus/NSO tools (2022)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

MP Lisa Hepfner moved that ETHI affirm it is satisfied the RCMP is not using Pegasus or NSO Group technology and supports the RCMP. Debate arose; MP Matthew Green moved to adjourn; the vote was 5-5 and the presiding Chair voted in favour, so the committee adjourned without voting on the motion. What stopped it: Adjournment motion carried on the Chair's deciding vote.
