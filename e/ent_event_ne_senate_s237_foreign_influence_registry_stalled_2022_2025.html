Senate Bill S-237 (foreign influence registry) never reached a second-reading vote (2022-2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Senator Leo Housakos's Bill S-237 was introduced Feb 24, 2022; LEGISinfo shows it 'At second reading in the Senate' with latest debate May 16, 2023. On the Senate Order Paper of June 19, 2024 it still sat as 'Resuming debate on the motion of the Honourable Senator Housakos… for the second reading of Bill S-237' with debate adjourned in the name of Senator Clement. Prorogation on Jan 6, 2025 ended the session. What stopped it: Second-reading debate stood adjourned for more than a year and a half, then the session was prorogued.
