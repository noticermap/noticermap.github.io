Outdoor event at Queen's Park, Toronto for the 70th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China (Sept 28, 2019), with the PRC consul general speaking

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Epoch Times reports that an outdoor event at Queen's Park, Toronto on Sept 28, 2019 marked the PRC's 70th anniversary. PRC Consul General Han Tao spoke, and Ontario NDP MPPs Peter Tabuns (who spoke) and Chris Glover attended. The article attributes the organization of that month's Toronto-area events to CTCCO president Weng Guoning; it does not separately name an organizer for this event.
