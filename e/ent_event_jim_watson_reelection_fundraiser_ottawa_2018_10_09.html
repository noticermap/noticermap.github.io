$250 re-election fundraiser for Mayor Jim Watson, Ottawa (Oct 9, 2018)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

CBC reported that Mayor Jim Watson's re-election campaign was selling tickets, at a $250 'suggested contribution', to a 5–7 p.m. fundraiser on Oct 9, 2018, the same evening as a mayoral debate organized by more than 40 community associations that Watson missed. Watson later tweeted that he attended a community association AGM that evening.
