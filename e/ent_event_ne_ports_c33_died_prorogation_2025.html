Ports c33 died prorogation (2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The government's port-reform bill from the Ports Modernization Review (amending the Canada Marine Act, Marine Transportation Security Act and Customs Act) stalled at House report stage after one day of debate on Sept 20, 2024. It died when Parliament was prorogued on Jan 6, 2025, never reaching third reading or the Senate. What stopped it: LEGISinfo: 'Report stage Not completed'; 'Third reading Not reached'; Senate stages 'Not reached'; session Nov 22, 2021 to Jan 6, 2025. The Proclamation prorogued Parliament to March 24, 2025.
