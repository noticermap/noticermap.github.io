CSIS overseas operation halted mid-course, then resumed after delay (2022)

Public records Sam Cooper / The Bureau ◔Listed in public records

Public records NSIRA found that the decision to halt an active CSIS operation abroad was not made by the CSIS Director, that there was no written record of a direction from the Minister, and that the halt "created unnecessary danger for the CSIS team"; the operation proceeded after a delay (NSIRA; CBC). NSIRA does not name who halted it. CBC reported that in Sept 2022 then-Public Safety Minister Marco Mendicino asked NSIRA to review the episode.

Sam Cooper / The Bureau The Bureau reports that an intelligence review found senior political actors around Prime Minister Justin Trudeau unlawfully intervened to halt a high-risk CSIS operation abroad (per The Bureau).
