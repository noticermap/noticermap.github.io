Saskatchewan government declines an NDP call for a judicial inquiry into Global Transportation Hub land purchases, and PAC defeats a motion to question officials (Mar-Nov 2016)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In March 2016 NDP Leader Cam Broten called for a judicial inquiry into GTH/Regina Bypass land purchases. He cited a Ministry of Highways appraisal far below the price paid. The government declined, and Cabinet instead asked the Provincial Auditor (Feb 9, 2016) to examine whether proper procedures were followed and appropriate value received. The Auditor's report found the GTH paid too much. On November 8, 2016 at the Public Accounts Committee, NDP member Cathy Sproule moved to have deputy economy minister Laurie Pushor and the current and former GTH CEOs appear. The motion was defeated. Premier Brad Wall said the four individuals had already been questioned in the audit (Global News/CP). Police investigated; this pass did not check whether that led to charges. Stopped (as reported): No judicial inquiry called; PAC witness motion defeated (by: Government of Saskatchewan (Saskatchewan Party); government members of PAC).
