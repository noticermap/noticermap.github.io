Arm's-length Alberta Environmental Monitoring, Evaluation and Reporting Agency (AEMERA) dissolved and folded into Alberta Environment and Parks (Bill 18, June 2016)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

AEMERA was created under the Protecting Alberta's Environment Act as an arm's-length agency to monitor and report on environmental conditions, notably in the oil sands. After Paul Boothe's review called it a 'failed experiment', Environment Minister Shannon Phillips announced on April 5, 2016 that monitoring would move into the department, overseen by a chief scientist (CBC). AEMERA's 2015-16 annual report says Bill 18 received royal assent May 27, 2016, repealing the Protecting Alberta's Environment Act, and AEMERA was dissolved effective June 30, 2016. Its functions went to a new Environmental Monitoring and Science Division. Stopped (as reported): Bill 18 repealed the Protecting Alberta's Environment Act; AEMERA dissolved effective June 30, 2016 (by: Government of Alberta (NDP), Minister of Environment and Parks Shannon Phillips; Legislature (Bill 18)).
