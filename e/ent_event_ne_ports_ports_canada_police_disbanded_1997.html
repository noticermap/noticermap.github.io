Ports ports canada police disbanded (1997)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Canada's national port police (324 officers at its 1972 peak, per the Senate) was eliminated in 1997 and policing was devolved to local forces. In the House, Transport Minister Collenette defended the move as devolution and promised that 'crime will not increase'. What stopped it: The force was disbanded in 1997. In Question Period, Elsie Wayne asked why the minister was 'disbanding the Ports Canada police'. Collenette replied that it was devolution and 'We put this regime in place.'
