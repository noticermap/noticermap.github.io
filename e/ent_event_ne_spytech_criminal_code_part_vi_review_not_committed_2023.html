Government did not commit to a Criminal Code Part VI review after ETHI Report 7 (2023)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

ETHI Report 7 Rec 3 called for a review of Criminal Code Part VI (invasion of privacy / interception) in light of ODITs. The government response did not commit to a review and said it was 'receptive to further potential reforms'. What stopped it: No review was committed in the response.
