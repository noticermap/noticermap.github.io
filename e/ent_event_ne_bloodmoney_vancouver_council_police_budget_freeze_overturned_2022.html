Vancouver council's freeze of the 2021 police budget was overturned by B.C.'s Director of Police Services

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In December 2020 Vancouver council declined to increase the Vancouver Police Department's 2021 budget. The Vancouver Police Board appealed under s. 27(3) of the Police Act; in March 2022 the provincial Director of Police Services ordered about $5.7 million restored. Mayor Kennedy Stewart called it 'the extraordinary removal of local democracy from the oversight of police' (CityNews). Stopped (as reported): Director of Police Services sided with the board (March 2022) and ordered funding restored; on April 12, 2022 council paid from reserves; Coun. Jean Swanson's motion to reject the decision did not pass (by: B.C. Director of Police Services on appeal by the Vancouver Police Board).
