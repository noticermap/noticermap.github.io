British Newfoundland Corporation (Brinco)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Consortium formed in 1953 under the leadership of N M Rothschild & Sons to develop land and resources in Newfoundland and Labrador (archive: 'led a syndicate to develop 60,000 square miles of land in Newfoundland'). Founding shareholders per the New York Times (1964): N M Rothschild & Sons, Anglo American Corporation of South Africa, Anglo-Newfoundland Development Co., Bowater Paper, English Electric, Frobisher Ltd and Rio Tinto. Developed the Hamilton (later Churchill) Falls power station, completed 1973.

Also known as: Brinco, British Newfoundland Corporation Limited
