Alberta Bill 14 ends the court reference on the independence referendum question (Dec 2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Chief electoral officer Gordon McClure referred the proposed independence question to the Court of King's Bench. Bill 14, tabled Dec 4, 2025 by Justice Minister Mickey Amery, would end that proceeding; Justice Colin Feasby ruled Dec 5 that the proposal would be unconstitutional (CBC). Bill 14 then passed, removing constitutional vetting and the CEO's power to refer; CBC says it made the ruling moot.
