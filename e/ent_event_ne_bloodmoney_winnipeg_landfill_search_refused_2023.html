Manitoba refused to support a Prairie Green landfill search for the remains of Morgan Harris and Marcedes Myran

Public records ◔Listed in public records

An Indigenous-led feasibility committee found a search of the Prairie Green landfill feasible, at up to three years and $184M with no guarantee of finding remains. On July 5, 2023 Premier Heather Stefanson told Indigenous leaders and those calling for the search that the province would support a memorial but not a search, citing worker safety. Winnipeg police had earlier declined to search (CityNews, Dec. 2022). Stopped (as reported): Province declined to fund or support a search (July 5, 2023) (by: Government of Manitoba (Premier Heather Stefanson)).
