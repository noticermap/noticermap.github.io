RCMP privacy impact assessment for device investigative tools delayed until 2023

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Per ETHI Report 7, the RCMP had used ODITs since at least 2017 (CAIT established 2016 per Q-566) and began drafting a PIA only in 2021; D/Commr. Bryan Larkin confirmed in Aug 2022 that no PIA had been completed, and the OPC said it was not consulted. Per OPC issue sheets, the OPC received the PIA on Sept 12, 2023, gave advice in Dec 2023, and found it 'conducted at a very high level which made it difficult to assess privacy risks.' What stopped it: The PIA was not complete at the Aug 2022 hearing and reached the OPC only on Sept 12, 2023.
