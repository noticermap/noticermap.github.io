Ontario NDP $9,975-a-plate private dinner with Andrea Horwath and Alberta Premier Rachel Notley, Toronto (Feb 19, 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Ontario NDP Leader Andrea Horwath hosted an intimate fundraising dinner for 20 invited members of the business and labour communities at a Toronto hotel restaurant; tickets were $9,975 (CBC). Alberta Premier Rachel Notley was a guest; tickets billed it as an evening with Horwath but donors were told beforehand Notley would attend. The Ontario NDP paid Notley's travel and kept all proceeds. Alberta reporters had been told Notley was going to Toronto to accept an award, which did not take place (CP via Globe). Ethics Commissioner Marguerite Trussler cleared Notley but warned about optics.
