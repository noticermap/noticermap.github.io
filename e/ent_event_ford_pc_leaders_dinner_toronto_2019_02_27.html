Ontario PC 2019 Leader's Dinner with Premier Doug Ford, $1,250 a plate, Toronto (Feb 27, 2019)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Ontario PC 2019 Leader's Dinner at the Toronto Congress Centre charged $1,250 a ticket. Ford said the party sold more than 3,200 tickets and called it 'the largest fundraiser in Canadian history'; CTV reported over 3,100 attended and an estimated $4 million raised. The Globe and Mail reported the party enlisted registered lobbyists to sell tickets and that some companies and industry groups felt pressure to attend; reporters were barred. Opposition parties called it cash-for-access; Ford and House Leader Todd Smith denied it.
