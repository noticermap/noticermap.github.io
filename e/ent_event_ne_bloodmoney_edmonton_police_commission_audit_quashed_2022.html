Edmonton council shelved, in a closed session, its auditor's proposed audit of the police commission and police service

Public records ◔Listed in public records

City Auditor Hoa Quach proposed a scope for reviewing the Edmonton Police Commission and the Edmonton Police Service's $384.8M 2022 budget. After going in camera for legal advice on Monday, Jan. 24, 2022, council did not vote on the audit, and no reason was given in public (Edmonton Journal). Stopped (as reported): After an in-camera session, council took no vote and gave no direction. The mayor's office said any revised audit would need the commission's consent to share records (by: Edmonton City Council (closed session)).
