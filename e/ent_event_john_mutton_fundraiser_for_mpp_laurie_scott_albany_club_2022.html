Fundraiser organized by lobbyist John Mutton for PC MPP Laurie Scott, Albany Club, Toronto (about Mar 1, 2022)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Trillium, citing the Ontario integrity commissioner's 2025 report on John Mutton, reports Mutton planned a fundraiser for PC MPP Laurie Scott held around March 1, 2022 at the Albany Club in Toronto that raised over $25,000 for her riding association, while he sought MZOs in her riding; Housing Minister Steve Clark granted the Kawartha Downs MZO on March 4, 2022. The commissioner made no formal ruling on Scott. Mutton received a two-year lobbying ban.
