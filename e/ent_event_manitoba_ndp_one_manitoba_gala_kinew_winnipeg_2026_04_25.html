Manitoba NDP One Manitoba Gala with Premier Wab Kinew, Winnipeg (Apr 25, 2026)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Manitoba NDP's event page advertised its flagship One Manitoba Gala with Wab Kinew for Saturday, April 25 [2026] in Winnipeg: a VIP reception with Kinew at 5 p.m. and cocktails at 6 p.m. The live page is headed 'SOLD OUT! Thank you for your support!' CBC News reported that Premier Kinew 'told 900 people at an NDP fundraising gala in Winnipeg on Saturday night' (Apr 25, 2026), where he announced a planned ban on social media and AI chatbots for youth. DiscoverWestman and NOW Toronto also reported the announcement at an NDP fundraiser on Saturday, April 25. CBC does not use the gala's name; the link rests on the matching date, city and organizer.
