Liberal $1,500 fundraiser with PM Justin Trudeau at Miaofei Pan's home, West Vancouver (Nov 7, 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Globe and Mail reported that developer Miaofei Pan hosted a $1,500-a-ticket Liberal fundraiser at his West Vancouver home on Nov. 7, 2016, where more than 80 guests dined and were photographed with Prime Minister Justin Trudeau. Pan told the Globe he lobbied Trudeau on investment in seniors' care, investor-immigration rules and foreign real-estate investment. The event was not listed by the PMO or party. The Tyee and iPolitics reported Pan's account (to a Wenzhou newspaper) that Trudeau's invitation to a dinner for Premier Li Keqiang prompted the fundraiser; the Liberal Party called that 'categorically false'.
