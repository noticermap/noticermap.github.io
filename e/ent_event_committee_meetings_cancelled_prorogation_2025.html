House committee meetings and government bills ended by prorogation (Jan 2025)

Wiretap Public records

On Jan 6, 2025 Parliament was prorogued to March 24 (SI/2025-9) on the advice of Prime Minister Justin Trudeau, who announced his resignation the same day. The Speaker's office said all scheduled committee meetings would be cancelled and government bills without Royal Assent would cease to exist.
