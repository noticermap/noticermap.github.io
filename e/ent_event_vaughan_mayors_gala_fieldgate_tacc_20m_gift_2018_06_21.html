City of Vaughan Mayor's Gala, where Mayor Bevilacqua announced a $20M gift from the TACC and Fieldgate founders' families (June 21, 2018)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

A Mackenzie Health release says a $20 million joint gift from the De Gasperis (TACC Group) and Kohn (Fieldgate Group) families for the new Mackenzie Vaughan Hospital was announced at the City of Vaughan's Mayor's Gala on June 21, 2018, by Mayor Maurizio Bevilacqua, co-chair of the hospital foundation's campaign cabinet. Tom Kohn, quoted on behalf of Fieldgate, links the gift to the Fieldgate Cares Foundation and congratulates the mayor.
