CIJA / Calgary Jewish Federation 'Spuds and Suds' Stampede Breakfast with Premier Danielle Smith, Calgary (July 7, 2023)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Alberta Jewish News reports that the Spuds and Suds Stampede Breakfast on July 7, 2023, a collaboration of CIJA, CJPAC, Calgary Jewish Federation and Jewish Federation of Edmonton, was a 'meet and greet' with elected politicians. Attendees included Premier Danielle Smith, NDP caucus chair Joe Ceci, Mayor Jyoti Gondek, councillors, MLAs and MPs. A Jewish Federation of Edmonton staffer is quoted saying Smith 'made it a priority to attend'.
