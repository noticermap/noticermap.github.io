House anti corruption committee defeated (2020-10-21)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Erin O'Toole moved, seconded by Gérard Deltell, that a special committee (the 'Anti-Corruption Committee') be appointed to examine matters including the Canada Student Service Grant, WE Charity's relationships with the government, the Canada Emergency Commercial Rent Assistance program and lobbying-related allegations. An amendment was negatived (Division No. 10, 143-181) and the main motion was negatived (Division No. 11, 146-180). What stopped it: Amendment negatived (Division No. 10, YEAS 143, NAYS 181); main motion negatived (Division No. 11, YEAS 146, NAYS 180).
