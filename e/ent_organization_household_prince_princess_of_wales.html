The official household of the Prince and Princess of Wales (William and Catherine). Sean Carney has been its chief operating officer since October 2023 (CBC, POLITICO, Duchy of Cornwall). Not Prince Andrew's household. Kept separate from any royal-household parent body (no subsidiary merge).
