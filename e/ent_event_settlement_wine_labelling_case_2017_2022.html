Settlement-wine labelling case before the CFIA and federal courts (2017–2022)

Breach ◑Partly verified

A 2017 complaint by David Kattenburg that West Bank settlement wines were labelled 'Product of Israel' led to CFIA rulings and Federal Court and Court of Appeal decisions (2019, 2021); the CFIA ruled again for accurate labelling in 2022 but, per The Breach, has not acted.
