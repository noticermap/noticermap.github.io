No federal moratorium on police facial recognition after ETHI Report 6

Public records ◔Listed in public records

ETHI Report 6 Rec 18 called for a federal moratorium on facial recognition by federal police and industry unless done with OPC consultation or judicial authorization. The government response 'acknowledges' it, says the Privacy Act already governs federal institutions, and points to Privacy Act modernization and the proposed CPPA (Bill C-27); no moratorium is committed. What stopped it: The response acknowledged the recommendation without imposing a moratorium.
