Ports rcmp port policing not restored

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In 2003 the Senate recommended re-creating the RCMP Marine Division, at least the former Ports Canada Police's size, to police major ports. By 2007 Transport Canada was still 'reviewing the issue of port policing'. The RCMP said about 900 personnel were needed, and the Committee found the RCMP 'has not even been adequately funded' to staff Halifax, Montreal and Vancouver. What stopped it: Seaports 2007: TC response says port policing is 'currently reviewing'; the Committee says 'The RCMP has not even been adequately funded to put meaningful contingents of officers at the ports of Halifax, Montreal, and Vancouver'. RCMP Asst. Commr. Souccar estimated '900 resources' were needed.
