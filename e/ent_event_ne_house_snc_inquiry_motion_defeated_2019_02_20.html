House SNC-Lavalin inquiry motion defeated (2019-02-20)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Charlie Angus's opposition motion, seconded by Pierre-Luc Dusseault, called on the Prime Minister to waive solicitor-client privilege for the former Attorney General regarding the SNC-Lavalin prosecution matter and urged a public inquiry under the Inquiries Act. An amendment setting a May 31, 2019 reporting date was negatived (Division 991, 133-159) and the main motion was negatived (Division 992, 134-160). What stopped it: Amendment negatived (Division No. 991, YEAS 133, NAYS 159); main motion negatived (Division No. 992, YEAS 134, NAYS 160).
