Supply of gold coin to Wellington's army for the British Government (1814-1815)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

N M Rothschild was commissioned by the British Government to supply Wellington's troops with gold coin in 1814 and 1815 (archive FAQ). The archive says the story that Nathan made 'millions' from early news of Waterloo rests on slender evidence and that any gain 'must have been very considerably less than a million pounds'; no evidence for pigeon post.
