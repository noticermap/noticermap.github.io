Crown declined charges against Vancouver officers after B.C.'s IIO forwarded a report on Myles Gray's 2015 death

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Myles Gray died after an encounter with Vancouver police in Burnaby in August 2015. The Independent Investigations Office sent a report to Crown counsel for consideration of charges in January 2019. The B.C. Prosecution Service announced no charges on Dec. 16, 2020. The OPCC then ordered a Police Act investigation (CBC). Stopped (as reported): BCPS clear statement: no charges approved (by: B.C. Prosecution Service (charge assessment)).
