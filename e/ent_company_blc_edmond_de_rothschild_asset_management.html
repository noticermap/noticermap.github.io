BLC-Edmond de Rothschild Asset Management Inc.

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Montreal fund manager (CBCA 361215-5, 1999-07-19), a joint venture of Laurentian Bank of Canada and La Compagnie Financière Edmond de Rothschild Banque; manager and promoter of the 'R' mutual funds (established 2 Nov 1999); described in 2003 as a majority-owned subsidiary of Laurentian Bank. Sold to Industrielle Alliance in 2005 (secondary source). Directors on the last registry filing include Henri-Paul Rousseau and Michel Cicurel.

Also known as: BLC - Edmond de Rothschild Gestion d'actifs Inc., BLC-Rothschild
