ETHI SNC-Lavalin study refused (2019-03-26)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Peter Kent moved that ETHI immediately begin a study of what the motion called the ongoing corruption scandal involving the Prime Minister, that the Chair write to the Prime Minister requesting he waive constraints, that Jody Wilson-Raybould and Jane Philpott be invited by April 5, and that the Committee report to Parliament. A Tracey Ramsey amendment was negatived 3-6 and the motion was negatived 3-6. What stopped it: Motion negatived on recorded division 3-6 (YEAS: Gourde, Kent, Ramsey; NAYS: Erskine-Smith, Fortier, de Burgh Graham, Lauzon, Picard, Saini).
