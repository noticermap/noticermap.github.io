Cyber security C-26 ended prorogation (2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Government Bill C-26, An Act respecting cyber security, amending the Telecommunications Act and making consequential amendments to other Acts, sponsored by the Minister of Public Safety, Democratic Institutions and Intergovernmental Affairs, was introduced in the 44th Parliament, 1st session. LEGISinfo records its final status for that session (November 22, 2021 to January 6, 2025) as 'At consideration in the House of Commons of amendments made by the Senate' (latest activity: Message sent to the House of Commons on Thursday, December 5, 2024 (Senate); House third reading completed June 19, 2024); it did not reach Royal Assent. The session ended with the Proclamation Proroguing Parliament to March 24, 2025 (SI/2025-9, January 6, 2025). What stopped it: Prorogation of Parliament on January 6, 2025 ended the session with the bill still unfinished.
