Government response to ETHI Report 13 on device-extraction tools not tabled before prorogation (2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

ETHI Report 13 on federal use of device-extraction tools was presented Oct 10, 2024 with a request for a government response (due within 120 days, around early Feb 2025). Parliament was prorogued Jan 6, 2025 (SI/2025-9); the study page lists no government response. What stopped it: Prorogation on Jan 6, 2025 ended the session before the response deadline; no response is listed.
