Quebec SAAQclic privilege motions rejected (2025-04)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On April 8, 2025 the Official Opposition House Leader, Monsef Derraji, moved under Standing Orders 324 and 327 that the Commission de l'Assemblée nationale be mandated to examine whether the SAAQ and certain current and former executives intentionally gave erroneous information to the Commission de l'administration publique about the CASA/SAAQclic project; the referral motion was rejected 34-76. On April 9, 2025 a deferred vote on his motion that the Assembly declare that the SAAQ and certain executives breached the rights and privileges of the Assembly by intentionally transmitting erroneous information was rejected 31-75. What stopped it: Referral motion rejected (Pour 34, Contre 76); privilege motion rejected (Pour 31, Contre 75).
