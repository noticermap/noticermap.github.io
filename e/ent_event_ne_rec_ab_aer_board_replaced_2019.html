Alberta Energy Regulator board chair and directors rescinded and replaced by an interim board chaired by a deputy minister (O.C. 156/2019, Sept 2019)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

O.C. 156/2019 (Sept 6, 2019), recommended by the Energy and Environment ministers under s.5 of the Responsible Energy Development Act, rescinded Sheila O'Brien's appointment as chair and the appointments of directors Brian Fleck, Monica Norminton and Jack Royal. It appointed an interim board to June 5, 2020: chair Beverly Yee (deputy minister of Environment and Parks, unpaid) with Sherri Brillon, David Goldie, Georgette Habib and Grant Sprague (King's Printer). Energy Minister Sonya Savage and Environment Minister Jason Nixon also launched a review of the AER's mandate. Media reported the board replacement and review followed a UCP campaign commitment (CBC, Global, Calgary Herald). Stopped (as reported): Cabinet order rescinded the chair's and three directors' appointments and installed an interim board (by: Lieutenant Governor in Council on recommendation of the Ministers of Energy and of Environment and Parks).
