Liberal Laurier Club $1,500 fundraiser with Finance Minister Bill Morneau at Fred George's home, Halifax (Oct 13, 2016)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Globe and Mail reported that about 15 business executives paid $1,500 each to attend a Liberal Laurier Club event with Finance Minister Bill Morneau at the Halifax waterfront home of mining magnate turned developer Fred George. Named attendees included developers Danny Chedrawe and Jim Spatz (recently appointed to the Halifax Port Authority board) and Philip Jenkins of RBC Securities. NDP Leader Tom Mulcair raised it in Question Period. Democracy Watch's compiled 2016 list records a National event with Morneau in Halifax on Oct. 13, 2016 at $1,500.
