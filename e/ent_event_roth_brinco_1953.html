Brinco consortium and Newfoundland land and water rights (1953)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

N M Rothschild & Sons led the syndicate that formed Brinco; in 1953 Brinco received extensive land and water rights from the province (archive timeline; Baker). Premier Smallwood met Edmund de Rothschild in London in 1952 and returned with 'Rothschild's promise to form a consortium' (Baker).
