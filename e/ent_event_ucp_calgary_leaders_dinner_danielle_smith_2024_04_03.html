UCP second annual Calgary Leader's Dinner with Premier Danielle Smith, $500 a plate (Apr 3, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The United Conservative Party held its second annual Calgary Leader's Dinner with Premier Danielle Smith at the BMO Centre on April 3, 2024. The Calgary Herald reported the sold-out $500-a-plate dinner drew more than 1,300 and, per organizers, raised $663,000, twice the previous year; the UCP said the prior year's event drew 950. CityNews reported over 1,350 attendees and estimated earnings topping $675,000. Smith gave the keynote. The Edmonton Leader's Dinner was set for April 16 at $350 a plate.
