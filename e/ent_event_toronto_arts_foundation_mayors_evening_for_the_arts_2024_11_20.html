Toronto Arts Foundation Mayor's Evening for the Arts hosted by Mayor Olivia Chow, Toronto (Nov 20, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Toronto Arts Foundation's release says its Nov 20, 2024 Mayor's Evening for the Arts was 'hosted by Mayor Olivia Chow' for the second consecutive year, aiming to raise more than $1 million; a public In Bloom Gala after-party sold $150 tickets. The foundation's page describes a VIP dinner for 'VIPs, City councillors and officials'.
