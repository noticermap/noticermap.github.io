UCP second annual Edmonton Leader's Dinner with Premier Danielle Smith, $350 a plate (Apr 16, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Edmonton Journal reported Premier Danielle Smith hosted the UCP's second annual Edmonton Leader's Dinner on Tuesday Apr 16, 2024 at the Edmonton Convention Centre, which the party said drew around 1,300 people. MLA Shane Getson told the crowd the event had raised $455,000, with the party hoping to add $100,000 via individual donations during the evening. Party president Rob Smith cited the $350-per-plate turnout. Before the event, the Calgary Herald reported it had sold 1,100 tickets at $350 a plate.
