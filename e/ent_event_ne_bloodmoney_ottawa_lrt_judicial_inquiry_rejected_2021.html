Ottawa council rejected a judicial inquiry into the Confederation Line LRT, 13-10

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Coun. Catherine McKenney twice sought a judicial inquiry into the procurement and launch of Ottawa's Confederation Line. An October 2021 attempt was blocked procedurally and replaced by an auditor-general probe; on Nov. 10, 2021 council voted 13-10 against an inquiry. (Ontario later called a public inquiry; not sourced here.) Stopped (as reported): Defeated 13-10; Mayor Watson and 12 councillors voted against, preferring the auditor general's non-public audit (by: Ottawa City Council majority (13-10), incl. Mayor Jim Watson).
