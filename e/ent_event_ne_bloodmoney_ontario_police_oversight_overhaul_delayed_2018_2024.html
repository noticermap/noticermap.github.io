Ontario paused the Safer Ontario Act's oversight overhaul in 2018; the replacement law took effect only in April 2024

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Liberal government passed the Safer Ontario Act in 2018, overhauling police oversight including the SIU and police boards. After winning power, Doug Ford's government paused its implementation, calling it anti-police. Its replacement, the Community Safety and Policing Act (2019), took effect on April 1, 2024, five years after passage. Stopped (as reported): Paused by the new government (2018). The CSPA, passed 2019, was in force April 1, 2024; the government cited bill size, regulations and COVID-19 (by: Government of Ontario (Premier Doug Ford) pausing the Safer Ontario Act). Canada Records Collector record (on-safer-ontario-act-police-oversight-never-proclaimed-2019): Ontario's Safer Ontario Act, 2018 (new Inspector General of Policing, Policing Oversight Act, expanded SIU) passed but its main schedules were never proclaimed; repealed in 2019 and replaced, with the new regime only in force in April 2024.
