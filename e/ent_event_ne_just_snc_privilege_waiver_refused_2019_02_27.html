JUST SNC-Lavalin privilege waiver refused (2019-02-27)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Murray Rankin moved that the Committee call upon the Prime Minister and Governor-in-Council to waive solicitor-client privilege and Cabinet confidentiality relating to the SNC-Lavalin issue so the former Attorney General could inform the Committee about the period after she ceased to serve as Attorney General on January 14, 2019. Debate was adjourned until questioning of the witness was completed; the motion was then negatived 4-5. What stopped it: Motion negatived on recorded division 4-5 (YEAS: Barrett, Cooper, Paul-Hus, Rankin; NAYS: Boissonnault, Ehsassi, Khalid, O'Connell, Sahota).
