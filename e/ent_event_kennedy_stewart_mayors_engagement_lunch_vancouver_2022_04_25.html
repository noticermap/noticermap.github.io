'Mayor's Engagement Lunch' fundraiser for Kennedy Stewart hosted by Francesco Aquilini, Vancouver (Apr 25, 2022)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Business in Vancouver reports that Canucks owner Francesco Aquilini invited business contacts to a fundraising lunch with Mayor Kennedy Stewart on April 25, 2022, 'with a focus on housing and development', with tickets at $600, $900 and $1,250. BIV later reported, citing Elections BC returns, that 54 people paid $1,250 and the campaign grossed $60,100.
