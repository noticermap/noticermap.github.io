Elections Alberta's 2025-26 supplementary funding request cut from $13.5M to $1.46M by Legislative Offices committee (Nov 2025)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Office of the Chief Electoral Officer asked for $13,512,118 in 2025-26 supplementary funding, including referendum preparation, recall petitions and citizen-initiative verification. UCP MLA Nolan Dyck moved to amend the request to $1,461,710; the motion carried 5-4 on a recorded vote (transcript). Dyck said much of the referendum cost was 12-18 months out and belonged in the spring budget; NDP members said it would slow processes the government had legislated. Stopped (as reported): Dyck amendment reducing the request to $1,461,710 carried 5-4 (by: Standing Committee on Legislative Offices (UCP majority)).
