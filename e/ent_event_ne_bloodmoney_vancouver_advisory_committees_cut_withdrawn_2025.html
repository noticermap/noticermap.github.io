Staff plan to end 10 of Vancouver's 11 council advisory committees early was pulled from the agenda before the vote

Public records ◔Listed in public records

City staff recommended ending the terms of 10 of 11 volunteer council advisory committees on Jan. 30, 2026 instead of Nov. 1, 2026, citing staff capacity during the World Cup and election year. The report was withdrawn less than a day before the council meeting, with no explanation given. Stopped (as reported): Report quietly withdrawn from the agenda; city said staff are 'considering alternative approaches' (by: City of Vancouver staff (report withdrawn from agenda)).
