Philippine Consulate General diplomatic reception for Independence Day, Vancouver (June 12, 2025)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Philippine Consulate General's own release says it hosted a reception on June 12, 2025 for the 127th anniversary of Philippine independence, with Vancouver Mayor Ken Sim, BC Speaker Raj Chouhan and Deputy Speaker Mable Elmore as guests of honour giving special messages. Sim presented a city proclamation; Elmore presented Premier David Eby's message (Eby is not reported as attending).
