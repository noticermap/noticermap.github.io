Alberta Ethics Commissioner Marguerite Trussler not reappointed; committee opts for open search six months after her report on the Premier (Nov 2023)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On May 17, 2023 Ethics Commissioner Marguerite Trussler found that Premier Danielle Smith contravened s.3 of the Conflicts of Interest Act in her interaction with the Justice Minister about a criminal case. On Nov 7, 2023, after an in-camera session on officer contracts, UCP MLA Glenn van Dijken moved that the Standing Committee on Legislative Offices recommend a select special committee to search for a new Ethics Commissioner; it passed on a voice vote. NDP MLA Marie Renaud spoke against it and said she had been ready to move Trussler's reappointment (transcript). Her contract expired in May 2024 (Globe and Mail). Stopped (as reported): Van Dijken motion recommending a select special committee to search for an Ethics Commissioner passed; Renaud's reappointment motion was not taken up because it was superseded (by: Standing Committee on Legislative Offices (UCP majority)).
