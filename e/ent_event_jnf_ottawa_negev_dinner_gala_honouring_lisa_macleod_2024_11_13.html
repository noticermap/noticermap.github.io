JNF Ottawa / Israel Magen Fund 2024 Negev Dinner Gala honouring MPP Lisa MacLeod, Ottawa (Nov 13, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

A listing on the Jewish Federation of Ottawa's calendar says the Jewish National Fund of Ottawa and the Israel Magen Fund of Canada would hold the 2024 Negev Dinner Gala honouring MPP Lisa MacLeod on Nov 13, 2024. The listing says MacLeod picked a project in Sderot, Israel to receive the dinner proceeds, names Warren Kinsella as keynote speaker, and calls the dinner 'a high-profile corporate event in Ottawa'.
