Jewish Community Centre of Greater Vancouver

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Community centre that was a client of consultant lobbyist Elly Alboim, Earnscliffe Strategy Group (registration 899538-363953, inactive; 2019-02-02 to 2023-04-03; client representative Eldad Goldfarb, Executive Director). Registered subject: federal funding for a new Jewish Community Centre development project. It also filed BC Lobbyists Registry registrations 1412-1855, 1583-1854, 7727-4062, 1121-1857, 2195-1856, 1571-1858 (2017-10-25 to 2022-03-22, all ended), seeking $50 million from the provincial and federal government for a community redevelopment.

Also known as: JCC Greater Vancouver, Jewish Comunity Centre of Greater Vancouver
