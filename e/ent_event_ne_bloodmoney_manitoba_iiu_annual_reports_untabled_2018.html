Manitoba's IIU 2016-17 annual report was never tabled until a Free Press series exposed it

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Independent Investigation Unit's 2016-17 annual report was delivered to Manitoba Justice but not made public. After a Winnipeg Free Press series found it missing, Justice Minister Cliff Cullen tabled it on Nov. 21, 2018, calling the omission 'an administrative error'. Stopped (as reported): Not tabled until Nov. 21, 2018; Cullen said the report was never received by his predecessor (by: Manitoba Justice (Minister Cliff Cullen)).
