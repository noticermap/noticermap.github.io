B.C. shelved the bill enabling dissolution of Vancouver's elected park board; the mayor later dropped the plan

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Mayor Ken Sim announced in December 2023 he wanted to abolish the elected Vancouver Board of Parks and Recreation. The province tabled enabling legislation Oct. 9, 2025, then said it would not call it for second reading after the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh Nations asked for a pause. Sim dropped the plan in July 2026 (CTV). Stopped (as reported): Minister Christine Boyle told Sim the bill would not be called for second reading after an MST Nations letter asked it be 'immediately paused'; Sim later abandoned dissolution (by: B.C.
