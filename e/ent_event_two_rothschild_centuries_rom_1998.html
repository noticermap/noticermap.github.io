'Two Rothschild Centuries' exhibition, Royal Ontario Museum (Nov 1998 - May 1999)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Travelling exhibition on Nathan Mayer Rothschild, shown at the ROM in Toronto from November 1998 to the end of May 1999, arranged by Kristen Peterson of Global Strategy Financial Inc. Sir Evelyn de Rothschild attended the gala fund-raising preview; guest of honour Hilary Weston, Lieutenant Governor of Ontario; fund-raising for Kids Help Phone and the ROM Foundation. Included 'images of key Canadian projects ... from railways ... to natural resources' and objects on Brinco lent by Edmund de Rothschild.
