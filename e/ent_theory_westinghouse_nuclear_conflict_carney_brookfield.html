'Nuclear conflict of interest': Brookfield's Westinghouse deal, Ukraine aid and Freeland (Hillier)

Theories (unverified) ◔Named in an unverified theory

Hillier says Brookfield sold Westinghouse 'to itself' via BGTF with about $4B profit extracted, that Canadian policy (Ukraine remission order, critical minerals strategy) and Ukraine aid route money to Cameco/Westinghouse reactors, and that Freeland's Ukraine role completes the loop. In uJMEBwH6G90 (2026-09-13) he adds that Carney's BGTF I carried interest rises with Westinghouse's value, so Canadian Ukraine aid, CUFTA and Freeland's Ukraine roles 'uplift' it, and that Carney walked away from a U.S. trade deal to protect it.
