CSIS threat-reduction measure against Indian intelligence networks scaled down (2017)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Per NSICOP's classified 2019 draft as quoted by The Bureau (inquiry exhibit TSC0000014): CSIS planned a 2017 threat-reduction measure against two alleged Indian intelligence networks but "scaled down its intervention on advice from Privy Council Office and Public Safety", did not engage elected officials or journalists as planned, and took no action on the Vancouver network; Global Affairs did not raise the diplomat's activities because of preparations for the Prime Minister's India trip.
