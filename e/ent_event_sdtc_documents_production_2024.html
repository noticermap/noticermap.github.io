House order for SDTC documents only partly complied with (2024)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The House adopted a Conservative motion June 10, 2024 ordering SDTC records to the law clerk for the RCMP. Some records were provided, but others were redacted or withheld, the government "citing privacy laws, solicitor-client privilege and cabinet confidence"; Speaker Greg Fergus found a prima facie question of privilege Sept 26, 2024, and debate on Andrew Scheer's motion stalled the House (CBC). Archive record: Partial disclosures, redactions and complete refusals by some institutions; Speaker found the order not fully complied with (prima facie privilege).
