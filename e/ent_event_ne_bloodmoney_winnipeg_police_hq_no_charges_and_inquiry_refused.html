After the RCMP's Winnipeg police HQ probe ended without charges, Manitoba refused the city's request for a public inquiry for more than five years

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In February 2017, council asked the province for a public inquiry into the police headquarters project and other real-estate and procurement matters. Premier Brian Pallister said there would be none while the RCMP investigated (Project Dalton). On Dec. 13, 2019 the Manitoba Prosecution Service authorized no charges, and Justice Minister Cliff Cullen said there would be no inquiry. In July 2023 the Stefanson government held that position, citing litigation. The province later called a focused police-HQ inquiry (CBC, 2025). Stopped (as reported): MPS: no charges ('not a reasonable likelihood of conviction'). Cullen: no inquiry (Dec. 13, 2019). Premier Heather Stefanson reaffirmed the refusal in July 2023 (by: Manitoba Prosecution Service (no charges); Pallister and Stefanson governments (inquiry refused)).
