Fortress Credit Co. LLC

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Lender to the Olympic Village developer. Under a Sept 28, 2007, agreement it made a $750M construction loan to MSEFC, and it stopped funding in Sept 2008. The City bought the loan in Feb 2009.
