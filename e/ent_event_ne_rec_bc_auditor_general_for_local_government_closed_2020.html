BC defunds and closes the Auditor General for Local Government a year after an independent review said it offered value; the Act is repealed in 2022

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On February 7, 2019 the province said the AGLG would continue to operate after an independent review concluded it offered value. The February 2020 budget cut its funding by 31 per cent, from $2.6M to $1.8M. Municipal Affairs Minister Selina Robinson said the office would be phased out and that she would work with UBCM, which had long opposed it (Vancouver Sun). The office stopped operations in March 2021 (BC Government), and the Auditor General for Local Government Act was repealed effective June 2, 2022 (SBC 2022, c. 15, s. 1). Stopped (as reported): Funding cut and phase-out announced; operations ceased March 2021; Act repealed June 2, 2022 (by: Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing Selina Robinson; Legislative Assembly (repeal)).
