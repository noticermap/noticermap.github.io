N.S. mass-shooting joint review panel replaced by a public inquiry (Jul 2020)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On July 23, 2020 Ottawa and Nova Scotia announced a joint review of the April 18-19, 2020 shootings; on July 28 Bill Blair announced a full Inquiries Act inquiry with the power to summon witnesses, with the same three panelists as commissioners (Public Safety Canada). It became the Mass Casualty Commission.
