MGERC finding on the CAF COVID-19 vaccination policy not adopted by the Final Authority (June 2024)

Wiretap

MGERC found the disputed provisions of the CAF COVID-19 vaccination policy unconstitutional and invalid (July 2023); in June 2024 the Chief of the Defence Staff, as Final Authority, disagreed in four grievance decisions, and MGERC maintained its finding (Revised Annex I, December 2024).
