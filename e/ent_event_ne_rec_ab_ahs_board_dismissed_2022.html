Alberta Health Services board (11 members) dismissed and replaced by a single official administrator, John Cowell (Ministerial Order 319/2022, Nov 2022)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Ministerial Order 319/2022 (Health), dated Nov 17, 2022, 'dismisses all current members of the Alberta Health Services Board and appoints an official administrator in place of the board' (Open Government). Premier Danielle Smith and Health Minister Jason Copping named Dr. John Cowell, who reported to the minister (CBC). Copping said the board would be restored 'at the appropriate time' (Edmonton Journal). AHS's 2022-23 annual report says that under s.11 of the Regional Health Authorities Act the administrator holds the board's governance functions and is accountable to the Minister and Premier. The order was reappointed by MOs 305/2023 and 307/2023. Stopped (as reported): MO 319/2022 dismissed all board members and appointed an official administrator (by: Minister of Health Jason Copping (ministerial order), on the Premier's leadership pledge).
