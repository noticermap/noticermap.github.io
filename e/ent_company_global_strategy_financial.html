Global Strategy Financial Inc.

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Toronto mutual-fund company. Rothschild Asset Management managed its international funds and 'owned 27% of Global' (Globe and Mail, 2005 retrospective); role cut to one-third of international mandates in Nov 1994; AGF bought Global Strategy in Aug 2000 for $438 million 'complete with the Rothschild connection'. Arranged the 1998-99 'Two Rothschild Centuries' exhibition at the ROM (archive bulletin).
