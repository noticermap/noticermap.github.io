GeoSpectrum Technologies Inc.

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Dartmouth, Nova Scotia company that designs underwater acoustic components and sonar systems. Elbit Systems Ltd. described it on 31 May 2018 as 'its wholly-owned subsidiary in Canada'. A 5 Dec 2018 GeoSpectrum release also called it a wholly owned subsidiary of Elbit Systems Ltd. A federal lobbying registration summary lists Elbit Systems Ltd. as parent. PSPC awarded it a $3,435,625 contract announced 7 Aug 2018 for Victoria-class submarine hydrophone spare parts. Later DND software and sonar contracts are listed in contracts.jsonl. A 19 Nov 2026 Saltwire article, quoting Yeatman, said the company does not design, manufacture or sell weapons; that is a company statement, not an independent finding.

Also known as: GeoSpectrum Technologies, GTI, GEOSPECTRUM TECHNOLOGIES INC., GEOSPECTRUM TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED
