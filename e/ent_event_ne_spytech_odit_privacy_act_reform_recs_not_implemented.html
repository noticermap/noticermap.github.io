ETHI Report 7 Privacy Act reforms (mandatory PIAs, OPC order powers) not implemented

Public records ◔Listed in public records

ETHI Report 7 (Nov 2022) recommended Privacy Act changes after the RCMP ODIT study: mandatory PIAs submitted to the OPC (Rec 1), privacy as a fundamental right (Rec 4), OPC order-making powers (Rec 6), privacy by design (Rec 7), and transparency (Rec 9). The government response (Mar 2023) said it was 'considering elevating to legislation' the PIA requirement and pointed to Justice's 2020 discussion paper and Bill C-27. The OPC told the Senate on Oct 14, 2025 that 'The Privacy Act... has remained unchanged since 1983', and its 2024-25 annual report says Bill C-27 'died on the order paper with the prorogation of Parliament in January 2025' and calls for 'long-awaited reform of the Privacy Act'. ETHI Report 13 (2024) repeated five of these recommendations. What stopped it: The government only committed to consider; per the OPC, the Privacy Act remained unchanged as of Oct 2025, and Bill C-27 (covering the PIPEDA side) died on prorogation.
