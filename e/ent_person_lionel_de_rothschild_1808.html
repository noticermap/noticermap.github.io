Lionel de Rothschild (1808-1879)

Head of N M Rothschild & Sons; MP for the City of London

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Elected for the City of London in 1847; took his seat in 1858 after the oath was changed. Arranged the 1875 £4m advance to the British Government for Suez Canal shares (archive).
