An Act to amend an Act of the present session entitled An Act to amend The Immigration Act

Immigration

Repeals and replaces s.15 of the same-session c.25, itself replacing 1910 Act s.41, concerning deportation for specified political conduct or affiliations; the exception specifies British subjects born or naturalized in Canada. Both c.25 and c.26 received assent June 6, not separate dates weeks apart.
