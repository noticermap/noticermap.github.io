Liberal 'donor appreciation event' (Laurier Club) with Justin Trudeau, Ottawa (Jun 18, 2018)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

National Observer, citing Liberal attendance records, reports Loblaw lobbyists Joanne Dobson (Summa Strategies) and Meredith Logan attended a large Liberal donor appreciation event in Ottawa on June 18, 2018 where party president Suzanne Cowan and Trudeau spoke, along with senior staff from Environment Minister Catherine McKenna's office, while Loblaw awaited a Low Carbon Economy Challenge grant (later $12 million). Loblaw and the lobbyists deny lobbying on the grant.
