Event · Nova Scotia

Promised independent probe of police conduct in Glen Assoun's wrongful conviction stalled for years after B.C.'s IIO withdrew

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In September 2020, Nova Scotia's justice minister asked SIRT to investigate possible police criminal misconduct before Glen Assoun's appeal. SIRT engaged B.C.'s IIO in March 2021. On April 18, 2023 the IIO withdrew, citing workload. By May 2025 no agency had taken over. An agreement with an Alberta team was later reported. Stopped (as reported): IIOBC withdrew on April 18, 2023 (workload and resources). The case was 'in limbo' as of May 2025 (by: IIO B.C. withdrawal; no replacement investigator until 2025).
