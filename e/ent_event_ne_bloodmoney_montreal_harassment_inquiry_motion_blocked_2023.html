Montreal council blocked an opposition motion for an independent inquiry into discriminatory-harassment complaint handling in the city public service

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Opposition leader Aref Salem sought to add item 65.03, a motion for an independent inquiry into how discriminatory-harassment complaints are handled and into the work climate in Montreal's public service. Under by-law 06-051 art. 32, adding the item needed unanimous consent, which was refused. A recorded vote failed 23-37. Stopped (as reported): Coun. François Limoges invoked art. 32 (unanimous consent). Majority leader Alex Norris said the administration did not support adding the item. The previous question was moved, and the recorded vote failed 23-37 (by: Montreal council majority (Projet Montréal) refusing unanimous consent; recorded vote 23-37).
