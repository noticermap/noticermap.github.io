JUST SNC-Lavalin witnesses refused (2019-02-13)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

At JUST meeting 129 the Committee agreed (5-4) to hold meetings on remediation agreements, the Shawcross Doctrine and discussions between the Office of the Attorney General and Government colleagues, with witnesses David Lametti, Nathalie Drouin and Michael Wernick. An amendment by Nathan Cullen to add Jody Wilson-Raybould, Gerald Butts and Mathieu Bouchard as witnesses and delete 'in camera' was negatived 4-5; an amendment by Michael Cooper calling on the Prime Minister to waive privilege was ruled out of order by the Chair as beyond the scope of the motion; and a separate Cooper motion asking the Prime Minister to immediately waive solicitor-client privilege involving the former Attorney General in respect of the SNC-Lavalin matter was negatived 4-5. What stopped it: Cullen amendment negatived on recorded division 4-5 (YEAS: Barrett, Cooper, Cullen, Raitt; NAYS: Boissonnault, Ehsassi, Fraser, Khalid, McKinnon); Cooper amendment ruled out of order; Cooper waiver motion negatived 4-5.
