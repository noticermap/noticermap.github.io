Ports mtscp held in reserve and narrowed

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Background checks for port workers were announced in January 2003 and recommended for all workers at major ports (SCONSAD 2002 Rec. 6). Transport Canada left the program out of the July 1, 2004 Marine Transportation Security Regulations ('held in reserve') and later narrowed it to designated duties and restricted areas. In April 2026 SECU testimony, a witness said under 20% of port workers hold clearances. What stopped it: 'Originally the MTSCP was to be included in the Regulations that came into force on July 1, 2004 ... However, to allow for more thorough consultations and policy development, it was held in reserve.' The program was then refocused on 'specific designated duties and smaller restricted areas'.
