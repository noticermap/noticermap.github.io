Rothschild Bank AG (Zurich)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Swiss private bank of the Rothschild & Co group (then Paris Orléans). Non-prosecution agreement with the U.S. DOJ under the Swiss Bank Program finalised 3 June 2015 (US$11.5 million). FINMA, 20 July 2018: Rothschild Bank AG and subsidiary Rothschild Trust (Schweiz) AG 'in serious breach' of Swiss anti-money-laundering rules in the 1MDB context (due diligence, reporting, documentation); audit agent appointed. Regulatory finding, not a criminal conviction.
