Justice committee ends SNC-Lavalin study before a second Wilson-Raybould appearance (Mar 2019)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

On March 19, 2019 the Liberal majority on the Commons Justice committee voted in camera to "consider the meetings on this topic to be concluded" (CBC) after 10 witnesses over five weeks; opposition MPs had sought a second appearance by Jody Wilson-Raybould. Archive record: Meeting adjourned mid-debate (5-4: YEAS Drouin, Ehsassi, Gerretsen, Lapointe, McKinnon; NAYS Barrett, Cooper, Poilievre, Ramsey); the Committee then declared its meetings on the topic concluded at an in camera meeting.
