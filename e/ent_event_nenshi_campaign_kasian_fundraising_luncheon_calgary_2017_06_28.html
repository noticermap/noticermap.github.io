Nenshi re-election campaign "intimate fundraising luncheon", $5,000 suggested per guest, Calgary (scheduled June 28, 2017; cancelled)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

CBC and the Calgary Herald reported that Mayor Naheed Nenshi's re-election campaign invited about 20 guests to an "intimate fundraising luncheon" on June 28, 2017, hosted by Kasian Architecture, with guests "encouraged to each donate $5,000" (Herald); the Herald's summary describes face time with the mayor "for a fee starting at $2,000". The campaign announced the cancellation late on June 26 after criticism the luncheon after criticism; Nenshi called the criticism "absolutely baseless and ridiculous".
