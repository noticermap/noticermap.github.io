Embassy of Japan National Day reception (Emperor's birthday), Ottawa (Feb 11, 2025)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

The Embassy of Japan says about 520 guests attended its Feb 11, 2025 national day reception, including Speaker Greg Fergus (remarks), Immigration Minister Marc Miller (toast) and Chief Government Whip Mona Fortier. Booths were provided by companies including Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Kawasaki Heavy Industries and All Nippon Airways.
