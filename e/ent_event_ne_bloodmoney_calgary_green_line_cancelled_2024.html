Calgary's Green Line LRT wound down after Alberta withdrew its $1.53B commitment

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Green Line, Calgary's largest-ever infrastructure project, had federal, provincial and city funding. On Sept. 3, 2024 Transportation Minister Devin Dreeshen wrote that the province would withdraw its $1.53 billion; on Sept. 17, 2024 council voted 10-5 to wind the project down. Stopped (as reported): Province withdrew funding (Dreeshen letter, Sept. 3, 2024); council voted 10-5 on Sept. 17, 2024 to wind down; Coun. Sonya Sharp's amendment to pause instead was defeated (by: Government of Alberta (Transportation Minister Devin Dreeshen) withdrawing funding; Calgary council 10-5 wind-down vote).
