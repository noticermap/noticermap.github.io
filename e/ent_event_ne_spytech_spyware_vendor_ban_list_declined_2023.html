Government declined to create a banned spyware vendor list after ETHI Report 7 (2023)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

ETHI Report 7 Rec 2 called for a list of banned spyware vendors and export-control rules. At meeting 33 Ronald Deibert (Citizen Lab) said 'there really are zero licensing or export controls in this country for the export or sale of spyware'. The government response did not create a banned-vendor list; it pointed to the existing Export Control List, the Export and Import Permits Act and the Export Controls and Human Rights Initiative. What stopped it: The government response declined to create a list and referred to existing export-control instruments.
