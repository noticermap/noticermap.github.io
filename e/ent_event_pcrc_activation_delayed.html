Public Complaints and Review Commission (RCMP/CBSA oversight) not yet brought into force

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Bill C-20 received Royal Assent on Oct 31, 2024 to create the Public Complaints and Review Commission, which Public Safety Canada called the first independent complaints and review body for the CBSA; its coming into force depends on an Order in Council, and Justice Laws still lists provisions not in force. The Globe and Mail and The Bureau reported in Aug 2026 that it had not been activated.
