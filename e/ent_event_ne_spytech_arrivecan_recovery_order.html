House order to 'collect and recoup' ArriveCAN funds within 100 days not met by the deadline (motion adopted Feb 28, 2024)

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Per Journals No. 287, the House adopted (170–149, Division No. 648) the opposition motion of Mr. Poilievre, seconded by Mr. Nater, including part '(b) call on the government to collect and recoup all funds paid to ArriveCAN contractors and subcontractors which did no work on the ArriveCAN app, within 100 days of this motion being adopted, and for the Prime Minister to table a report in the House demonstrating that taxpayer funds have been repaid.' At OGGO meeting 132 (Jul 23, 2024, block (1250)), Larry Brock said: 'One hundred days from February 28 would have been June 6. ... Nothing but crickets.' In an answer to Q-3138 (Hansard No. 389, Dec 13, 2024), Jennifer O’Connell said 'the CBSA is currently reviewing all invoices submitted by GC Strategies, Coradix Technology Consulting and Dalian Enterprises Inc. to determine whether any overpayments were issued'. What stopped it: Attributed only: Brock (OGGO 132, (1250)): 'One hundred days from February 28 would have been June 6. That passed over a month ago. ... Nothing but crickets.' O’Connell (Q-3138): 'the CBSA is currently reviewing all invoices submitted by GC Strategies, Coradix Technology Consulting and Dalian Enterprises Inc. to determine whether any overpayments were issued'.
