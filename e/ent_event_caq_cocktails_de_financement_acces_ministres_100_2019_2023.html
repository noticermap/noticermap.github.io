CAQ $100 cocktails de financement where mayors say they paid to meet ministers (2019–2023, series)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

Radio-Canada reported (Dec 12, 2023) that several Quebec mayors said they donated to the Coalition avenir Québec, typically $100, to attend partisan cocktail fundraisers and get a discussion with visiting ministers. Examples: a mayor met Minister Pierre Fitzgibbon this way after $100 donations in 2019 and 2021; Saint-Antonin mayor Michel Nadeau met Education Minister Bernard Drainville at a cocktail de financement in Rivière-du-Loup in June 2023. Premier François Legault's press attaché Ewan Sauves said no mayor is forced to attend. The PQ (Pascal Bérubé) proposed a bill to take ministers out of political fundraising.
