Edmonton Police Commission refused council's request for an EPS internal-audit plan and said a program-services review would not be done

Public records ◔Listed in public records

In December 2023, council asked the commission for an audit plan showing which parts of the Edmonton Police Service were being audited, and for a program-services review. In an April 19, 2024 letter, the commission refused to share internal audit plan information and said no program-services review would be undertaken (Global News). Stopped (as reported): Commission letter of April 19, 2024 declining both. Chair John McDougall said the commission 'already regularly audits the police service' (by: Edmonton Police Commission).
