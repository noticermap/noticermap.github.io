Coroner's inquest into Regis Korchinski-Paquet's 2020 death adjourned indefinitely just before closing submissions

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The inquest into the May 27, 2020 death of Regis Korchinski-Paquet, who fell from a 24th-floor balcony while Toronto police were in her home, finished hearing evidence and was due for closing submissions when the presiding coroner adjourned it 'until further notice' for an 'unanticipated investigation'. The SIU had earlier cleared police; that finding was disputed (CBC/CP). Stopped (as reported): Coroner Dr. David Cameron adjourned the inquest indefinitely, citing 'quite unanticipated investigation that needs to be done', with no details (by: Presiding coroner Dr. David Cameron).
