Grand Trunk Pacific Railway Company

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Western extension of the Grand Trunk Railway (Winnipeg to Prince Rupert), built with federal assistance under a 1903 agreement with the Government of Canada. N M Rothschild & Sons issued £3m 3% First Mortgage Sterling bonds for it in 1905 and a further £2m in 1909 (archive). Nationalised by 1919-1923 and merged into Canadian National Railways in 1923 (archive).
