BC NDP 'evening gala' with Premier David Eby and caucus, Surrey – postponed (scheduled Feb 4, 2024)

Events (fundraisers & galas) ◔Listed in the Events layer

CityNews reported the BC NDP postponed a fundraiser billed as an 'evening gala' with Premier David Eby and the NDP caucus, set for the evening of Sunday Feb 4, 2024 in Surrey, after multiple groups announced protests. Party spokesperson Heather Libby cited timing. Ticket sales were to fund NDP candidates' campaigns in Surrey. Event did not take place on that date.
