Crown stayed ASIRT's assault-causing-bodily-harm charge against Edmonton Const. Michael Zacharuk after obtaining an outside use-of-force opinion

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Everett Rain suffered a skull fracture in the Edmonton police cellblock in November 2019. ASIRT charged Const. Michael Zacharuk with assault causing bodily harm (ASIRT release, Feb. 17, 2022). The Crown later stayed the charge after receiving an opinion from use-of-force expert Brad Fawcett. In February 2025, Chief Dale McFee found a reasonable prospect of a misconduct finding but held no disciplinary hearing. Stopped (as reported): Crown stayed proceedings after an out-of-province use-of-force opinion (exact stay date not given in the fetched sources). Chief McFee's decision of Feb. 6, 2025 cited the stay in declining a hearing (by: Alberta Crown Prosecution Service (stay)).
