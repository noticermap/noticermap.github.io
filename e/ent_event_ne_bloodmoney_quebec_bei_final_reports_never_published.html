Quebec's BEI is the only police oversight body in Canada whose final investigation reports are never made public

Public records ◔Listed in public records

Ten years after it began (2016) and with about 490 investigations opened, the Bureau des enquêtes indépendantes still does not publish its final reports (CBC). Director Brigitte Bishop said the BEI's constituting act does not allow more transparency and that legislative changes are being worked on with the ministry. Stopped (as reported): BEI says its act does not permit publication. The minister's office did not respond to CBC (by: Quebec Police Act framework; Quebec government (no legislative change yet)).
