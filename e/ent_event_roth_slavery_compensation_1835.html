West India Loan: £15 million raised for slavery compensation (1835)

Public records ✓Verified in public records

In August 1835 a City syndicate led by Nathan Mayer Rothschild and Moses Montefiore raised £15 million to fund compensation paid to former slave-owners under the Slavery Abolition Act (Bank of England staff working paper).
