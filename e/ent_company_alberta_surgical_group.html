Alberta Surgical Group

Public records ✓Verified in public records

Proponent ranked first in both AHS Sept 7, 2022 RFPs for multi-modality Chartered Surgical Facilities (Central and South Zones); directors at Nov 22, 2022 were Dr. Les Scheelar, Dr. Darcy Durand and Dr. Ken Hawkins; by late Oct/early Nov 2023 it changed the vendor to numbered operating companies (Wyant report pp. 30-31). The report found no improper conduct by any minister, minister's staff or public servant in the CSF procurement (p. 30).

Also known as: Canadian Surgical and Medical Network (Alberta Surgical Group), Canadian Surgical and Medical Network, ASG
