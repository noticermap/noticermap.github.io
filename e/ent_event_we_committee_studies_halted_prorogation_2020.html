House committee studies of WE Charity halted by prorogation (Aug 2020)

Wiretap Public records

Prime Minister Justin Trudeau had Parliament prorogued from Aug 18 to Sept 23, 2020; CBC and the National Post reported this suspended the finance, ethics and government operations committee studies of the WE Charity student-grant contract. Conservative MP Pierre Poilievre called it a "coverup" (CBC). Law clerk Philippe Dufresne had been reviewing redactions in WE documents given to the finance committee (CBC).
