Ottawa police board's hiring of Matt Torigian as interim chief undone after council ousted board chair Diane Deans

Public records ◔Listed in public records

During the 2022 convoy occupation, after Chief Peter Sloly resigned, the Ottawa Police Services Board under chair Diane Deans engaged former Waterloo chief Matt Torigian as interim chief. Council voted 15-9 on Feb. 16, 2022 to remove Deans as chair; Torigian withdrew Feb. 17 and Steve Bell remained interim chief. Stopped (as reported): Council voted 15-9 to remove Deans as chair (motion by Scott Moffatt and Laura Dudas, per Ottawa Citizen); Eli El-Chantiry became chair; Torigian withdrew Feb. 17, 2022 (by: Ottawa City Council (15-9 vote removing board chair); Matt Torigian withdrawal).
