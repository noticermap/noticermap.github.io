B.C.'s all-party Police Act reform recommendations (2022) were largely unimplemented three years later, with no timeline

Public records ◔Listed in public records

The Special Committee on Reforming the Police Act reported on April 28, 2022 with 11 recommendations, including a provincial police service and First Nations police governance. In April 2025 critics said progress had stalled. Solicitor General Garry Begg said all recommendations would be implemented but gave no timeline. Stopped (as reported): Only Bill 17 (Police Amendment Act, April 2024) passed, which critics called 'transactional'. No timeline for the rest (by: Government of British Columbia (implementation not completed)).
