An Act respecting the Department of Immigration and Colonization

Immigration

Creates the statutory departmental/ministerial framework and transfers administration of the general and Chinese immigration Acts and related War Measures orders. Section 5 validates the minister’s exercise of powers since October 12, 1917; that reference is not this Act’s assent date.
